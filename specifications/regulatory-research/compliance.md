## 1. Product governance — S2, S11

### MiFID II

**Directive 2014/65/EU**, with **Delegated Directive (EU) 2017/593** carrying the product governance
detail. Transposed in Czechia by **Act No. 256/2004 Coll. on capital market undertakings (ZPKT)**; the
MiFID II amendment took effect **3 January 2018**.

The manufacturer identifies a target market at a sufficiently granular level, identifies groups of clients
for whose needs the product is not compatible, runs a scenario analysis, and reviews the product
periodically. The distributor obtains the manufacturer's target market, defines its own distribution
strategy, and reports back sales outside the target market.

**The ČNB has published its position on the MiFID II product governance rules** and has notified compliance
with the ESMA guidelines on product governance requirements. The expectation is consistent application of
target market and distribution strategy definition, periodic review, monitoring of out-of-target-market
sales, and manufacturer-distributor information flow.

Source: ČNB, *Pravidla vytváření a nabízení investičních nástrojů (product governance)*, RS2017-0015;
ČNB communication on the ESMA product governance guidelines.

### Retail banking — the EBA guidelines

**EBA/GL/2015/18**, Guidelines on product oversight and governance arrangements for retail banking
products, **applicable from 3 January 2017**. Scope: mortgages, personal loans, deposits, payment accounts,
payment services and electronic money. Requirements cover internal control functions, target market
identification, product testing, disclosure to consumers, product monitoring, remedial action and
distribution channels.

**Amended by EBA/GL/2026/07 of 30 June 2026.** The amendment clarifies requirements for products with
environmental, social and governance features offered to consumers, and addresses greenwashing risk. A
consolidated version was published by the EBA in June 2026.

**Why this matters to the card.** MiFID II product governance is often read as an investment-only concern.
The EBA guidelines put target market and product monitoring obligations on ordinary retail banking
products, which is most of what a discovery run in this pilot is about.

Sources: [EBA guidelines page](https://www.eba.europa.eu/activities/single-rulebook/regulatory-activities/consumer-protection/guidelines-product-oversight-and-governance-arrangements-retail-banking-products) ·
[consolidated EBA/GL/2015/18, June 2026](https://www.eba.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-06/8f6eeb6c-371d-4a96-ad85-f6c6d07142cc/Consolidated%20version%20of%20EBA-GL-2015-18%20on%20product%20oversight%20and%20governance%20.pdf) ·
[EBA/GL/2026/07 final report](https://www.eba.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-06/d4b262c5-d8b7-45fa-a772-89a3719b61cd/EBA%20amending%20GLs%20on%20POG%20GLs%20-%20Final%20report.pdf). Read 27 August 2026.

## 2. Investment products — S3, S6

**PRIIPs, Regulation (EU) No 1286/2014**, with **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/653**. Applied from
**1 January 2018**. A person who advises on or sells a packaged retail and insurance-based investment
product to a retail investor must supply the key information document **in good time before the transaction
is concluded**. Structured deposits fall in scope where they meet the definition.

**Suitability and appropriateness** sit in MiFID II Article 25 with **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/565**
Articles 54 to 56. Advice and portfolio management require suitability and a suitability report;
non-advised sales of complex instruments require appropriateness.

**Sustainability preferences** were added to the suitability assessment by **Delegated Regulation (EU)
2021/1253**.

Sources: [PRIIPs on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A32014R1286) ·
[EUR-Lex summary](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/key-information-about-investment-products.html). Read 27 August 2026.

## 3. Payments — S8

**PSD2, Directive (EU) 2015/2366**, transposed by Czech **Act No. 370/2017 Coll.** on payment services,
effective **13 January 2018**.

**Strong customer authentication** is Article 97 of PSD2, transposed at **§223 of Act No. 370/2017 Coll.**,
and **required in Czechia from 1 January 2021**. It applies where the payer accesses a payment account
online, initiates an electronic payment transaction, or performs any action carrying a risk of payment
fraud or misuse of a payment instrument or account information. The technical standard is **Delegated
Regulation (EU) 2018/389**.

**Payment Accounts Directive 2014/92/EU** carries the fee information document, the annual statement of
fees, comparability, switching and access to a basic account.

Sources: [Act No. 370/2017 Coll.](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-370) · [ČNB payments legislation](https://www.cnb.cz/cs/platebni-styk/pravni-predpisy/). Read 27 August 2026.

## 4. Consumer credit — S9

**Currently:** Directive 2008/48/EC, transposed by Czech **Act No. 257/2016 Coll.** on consumer credit.
Standard pre-contractual information, the annual percentage rate of charge, a mandatory creditworthiness
assessment, and a fourteen-day withdrawal right.

**From 20 November 2026: Consumer Credit Directive 2, Directive (EU) 2023/2225.** Published in the Official
Journal 30 October 2023, in force 19 November 2023. **Member States were to transpose by 20 November 2025
and to apply from 20 November 2026.** The Czech route is an amendment to Act No. 257/2016 Coll.

What changes: buy-now-pay-later and small short-term credit are brought into scope, pre-contractual
information is revised, online conclusion is addressed, the creditworthiness assessment is tightened, and
advertising is constrained.

**This is the nearest hard date in the whole four-area map.** An initiative involving credit scoped in
August 2026 will very likely deliver after it applies.

Sources: [Directive (EU) 2023/2225 on EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/2225/oj/eng) ·
[CCD2 Czech overview](https://www.ccd2.cz/en/) ·
[Havel & Partners on CCD2 transposition](https://en.havelpartners.blog/ccd2-transposition-doubling-down-on-consumer-protection). Read 27 August 2026.

## 5. Conduct and communications — S1, S4, S5

**MiFID II** Article 24(3) requires information to be fair, clear and not misleading; Article 24(4) sets
ex-ante and ex-post aggregated cost and charge disclosure; Articles 23 and 24(9) with **Delegated Directive
(EU) 2017/593** govern conflicts and inducements. **IDD** Articles 17, 27 to 29 carry the insurance
equivalents.

**Unfair Commercial Practices Directive 2005/29/EC** and Czech **Act No. 634/1992 Coll.** on consumer
protection apply across all of it, assessed by effect on the average consumer's transactional decision.

## 6. Sustainability — S10

**SFDR, Regulation (EU) 2019/2088** and the **Taxonomy Regulation (EU) 2020/852**.

**ESMA guidelines on funds' names using ESG or sustainability-related terms**, applying from 2025, require
a fund using such a term in its name to invest at least 80% in line with the characteristics or objectives
promoted. ESMA's own December 2025 analysis found two thirds of reacting funds renamed and more than half
changed investment policy.

**SFDR review, "SFDR 2.0", is in progress during 2026**, moving toward a simplified categorisation of
Sustainable, Transition and ESG Basics. **A sustainability claim made now may need re-labelling.**

Sources: [ESMA analysis of the naming guidelines, December 2025](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-12/ESMA50-801628861-4423_TRV_Risk_Analysis_Impact_of_the_ESMA_Guidelines_on_the_use_of_ESG_or_sustainability-related_terms_in_fund_names.pdf). Read 27 August 2026.

## 7. Accessibility — S13

**European Accessibility Act, Directive (EU) 2019/882**, transposed by Czech **Act No. 424/2023 Coll.** on
accessibility requirements for certain products and services, **effective 28 June 2025**.

**Banking services are expressly in scope.** The act reaches consumer banking services, e-commerce
services, websites and mobile applications, self-service terminals including ATMs, digital documents and
forms, and authentication and support journeys. It requires relevant banking identification, security and
payment functionality to be perceivable, operable, understandable and robust.

**The technical bar is EN 301 549**, which for web content aligns to **WCAG 2.1 level AA**.

Exemptions: micro-enterprises under ten employees, and disproportionate burden. **A disproportionate burden
claim is itself an assessed and documented artefact** rather than an opt-out.

Oversight: the **Ministry of Industry and Trade** holds the framework; the **Czech Trade Inspection
Authority** supervises services.

**Why this section is emphatic on the card.** Across the eight committed discovery runs in this repository,
every one is a client-facing digital journey and **not one names accessibility as a compliance
touchpoint**, while this act has been in force for over a year.

Sources: [MPO on Act No. 424/2023 Coll.](https://mpo.gov.cz/cz/podnikani/standardizace/pristupnost-vyrobku-a-sluzeb/zakon-c--424-2023-sb---o-pozadavcich-na-pristupnost-nekterych-vyrobku-a-sluzeb--279601/) ·
[EY tax alert, June 2025](https://www.ey.com/cs_cz/technical/tax/tax-alerts/2025/06/novy-zakon-o-pozadavcich-na-pristupnost-nekterych-vyrobku-a-sluzeb) ·
[Czech Banking Association accessibility material](https://www.cbaeducaplus.cz/accessibility/). Read 27 August 2026.

## 8. What is moving, with the evidence

### Retail Investment Strategy

Council and Parliament agreement **18 December 2025**; Member States approved **12 June 2026**; publication
in the Official Journal was expected **September 2026**.

Amends MiFID II, IDD, Solvency II, UCITS, AIFMD and PRIIPs. **No ban on inducements** in the final
compromise, but an enhanced inducements test where inducements are paid or received, with Member States
able to apply a ban at their discretion. Adds value-for-money assessment for manufacturers and
distributors, harmonised cost disclosure, strengthened rules on marketing including finfluencers, adviser
training including on ESG, and a modernised PRIIPs key information document.

Timing after publication: transposition at 24 months, most rules apply at 30 months, **PRIIPs rules at 18
months**.

Sources: [Council press release, 18 December 2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/18/retail-investment-strategy-council-and-parliament-agree-on-package-to-empower-consumers-while-boosting-markets/) ·
[Council policy page](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/retail-investment-strategy/). Read 27 August 2026.

### PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation

Political agreement **27 November 2025**. COREPER endorsement **22 April 2026**. ECON committee vote
**5 May 2026**, plenary expected later in May. Publication anticipated June or July 2026 with possible slip
to September. **The new rules generally apply 21 months after publication**; PSD3 transposition within 18
months of entry into force, with the PSR applying directly on the same date.

**As at 27 August 2026 the texts had not completed formal adoption and were not applicable law.** PSD2 and
Act No. 370/2017 Coll. continue to govern.

Sources: [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/cedd39c6/psd3-and-psr-from-provisional-agreement-to-2026-readiness) ·
[Morrison Foerster, 30 April 2026](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/260430-psd3-and-the-payment-services-regulation-key-developments). Read 27 August 2026.

### FiDA

Formal adoption expected around mid-2026, with obligations phased from 2027 as financial data sharing
schemes are established. Introduces a client-facing permission dashboard obligation.

Source: [Capco, FiDA primer for 2026](https://www.capco.com/intelligence/capco-intelligence/fida-primer-for-2026-and-beyond). Read 27 August 2026.

## 9. Where this dossier is thin

**ČS internal process is absent.** Which committee approves what, what the product approval process
requires, and what the bank's own product sheet looks like are not public and are not here. **The card
names expected artefacts and cannot name the process that produces them.**

**ČNB guidance is sampled, not surveyed.** Positions on product governance and on IPID scope were read.
The full body of ČNB opinions was not.

**No Czech-language primary statute was read end to end.** Act numbers, effective dates and the location of
key provisions were confirmed against Czech legal sources; the operative text was not analysed clause by
clause.

**The eight escalation-relevant judgements on the card are ČS's own** and this dossier does not second-guess
them.
