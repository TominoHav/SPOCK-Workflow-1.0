## 1. Consumer contracts in Czech law

**Act No. 89/2012 Coll., the Civil Code.** Obligations from contracts concluded with consumers sit at
**§1810 to §1867**. **Contracts for financial services concluded at a distance sit at §1841 to §1851.**

A financial service contract for that purpose covers banking, credit, payment and insurance services,
supplementary pension products, currency exchange, issuance of electronic money, investment services and
trading in investment instruments, and **contracts for the acquisition of crypto-assets or services
relating to them**.

**Pre-contractual information** must be given in text form and covers the provider's identity, the service,
the total price including all fees and taxes, the risks, means of payment, contract duration, the
supervisory authority, the withdrawal procedure and the dispute resolution route.

**Withdrawal:** fourteen days from conclusion, **thirty days for life insurance and supplementary pension
products where information was supplied after conclusion**. Named exceptions include contracts whose price
depends on financial market movements outside the provider's control, and short-term travel insurance.

Sources: [ČNB, distance financial services contracts](https://www.cnb.cz/cs/dohled-financni-trh/ochrana-spotrebitele/smlouvy-na-dalku/) ·
[Act No. 89/2012 Coll.](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2012-89) ·
[Ministry of Finance consumer protection material](https://financnigramotnost.gov.cz/cs/ochrana-spotrebitele/novy-obcansky-zakonik). Read 27 August 2026.

## 2. The live gap: Directive (EU) 2023/2673

**The new distance marketing regime for consumer financial services.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Published | Official Journal, 2023 |
| Transposition deadline | **19 December 2025** |
| Application | **19 June 2026** |
| Effect | **Repeals Directive 2002/65/EC from 19 June 2026** and inserts a financial services chapter into the Consumer Rights Directive |

What it adds: expanded pre-contractual information duties, rules on how information is presented online
including protection against dark patterns, safeguards where an online tool rather than a person answers
the consumer, and **a withdrawal function in the interface — a "withdrawal button"**.

**The Czech position as at 27 August 2026 could not be settled from public sources with confidence.** The
Ministry of Finance recorded an amendment on distance conclusion of financial services for consumers
advancing to second reading in the Chamber of Deputies during 2026, and separate reporting in August 2026
described an amendment approved by the Senate transposing the directive and introducing the withdrawal
button. **The EU application date has passed and the national implementation is not confirmed as in force.**

**This is a question for ČS legal, not something the workflow resolves.** It is on the card's *what is
moving* section rather than stated as settled, and an initiative concluding contracts at a distance should
raise it.

Sources: [Arthur Cox on the 19 June 2026 date](https://www.arthurcox.com/insights/distance-marketing-new-rules-published-in-official-journal-and-will-apply-from-19-june-2026/) ·
[Linklaters on the transposition deadline](https://www.linklaters.com/knowledge/articles/alerts-newsletters-and-guides/2025/november/05/eu-directive-on-distance-marketing-of-financial-services-one-month-to-the-transposition-deadline) ·

## 3. Unfair terms and consumer protection

**Civil Code §1810 onward**, with the transparency requirement that a term not clearly and comprehensibly
expressed is construed against the party that supplied it. **Act No. 634/1992 Coll.** on consumer
protection, and **Directive 2005/29/EC** on unfair commercial practices, which assesses a practice by its
effect on the average consumer's transactional decision. **That test reaches interface design, not only
copy.**

## 4. Signature, identification and evidence

**Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS)** with Czech **Act No. 297/2016 Coll.** on trust services. Three
levels of electronic signature, and the level required depends on the document and on what has to be proved
later.

**Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 (eIDAS 2)** adds the EU Digital Identity Wallet, with Member State wallets
expected during late 2026 and private-sector acceptance obligations roughly twelve months later.

**Identification, authentication and signature are three different acts** and a remote journey frequently
treats them as one. The card separates them because the legal consequence of each is different.

## 5. Disputes

**Act No. 229/2002 Coll.** establishes the **Financial Arbitrator**, whose competence covers payment
services, consumer credit, life insurance, collective investment and currency exchange among others. **The
client must be told the route exists.** The **Czech Trade Inspection Authority** covers general consumer
matters.

---

## 6. Perimeter, suppliers and intellectual property

**Act No. 21/1992 Coll. on banks** sets what a bank may do and on what permission. Outsourcing carries
contractual requirements from the **EBA Guidelines on outsourcing arrangements (EBA/GL/2019/02)**, and where
the dependency is ICT, from **DORA Article 30** — carried in the ICT area rather than duplicated here.

**Act No. 121/2000 Coll.** on copyright governs content, and licence scope has to cover the use actually
made rather than the use intended when the licence was bought.

what that means for a specific country was not worked out.
