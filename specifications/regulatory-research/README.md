# Regulatory research dossiers

**Date:** 27 August 2026 · **Status:** Evidence, not knowledge · **Owner:** Igor Tadic

> **What this is.** The statutory reading behind the four area cards in
> [specifications/definitions/know-how/compliance/](../definitions/know-how/compliance/). One file per area.
> Instruments, citations, what each one says, the sources consulted, and the dates they were read.
>
> **Nothing here is read by a producer, and that is the point.** The requirements for this work state
> that a summary of a regime written by us and read by a producer as authority would age badly and would
> put an unreviewed legal reading into documents ČS receives. **The area cards carry obligations in
> operational terms and cite the instrument. These files carry the reading and the reasoning.**
>
> **No skill declares any of these files** and none is in the know-how tree, so nothing in a run can
> retrieve them. They exist so a person can check where a statement on a card came from.

---

## Why the split exists

The Compliance area card ČS supplied names regimes and states no obligation. **A producer reading it alone
cannot tell what a rule asks for**, which is why the cards gained a *what the regimes oblige* section.
That section is a reading of public law, and a reading has to be checkable.

**The rule for what goes where.** If a producer changes what they build because of it, it belongs on the
card. If it explains why the card says what it says, it belongs here.

## Standing caveats

**One card is the bank's and three are ours.** Only the Compliance card came from ČS, and its own status is
unconfirmed. GDPR, Legal and ICT security were written here from public sources.

**This is not legal advice and no lawyer has reviewed it.** It is a structured reading assembled to make a
discovery workflow able to ask the right questions. **Any statement that would change a legal position
should be confirmed with ČS legal or compliance before it is relied on.**

**Secondary sources were used where a primary was impractical.** Law firm briefings and regulator summaries
were used to establish dates and status. Where a date matters, the primary instrument is cited alongside.

**These files go stale.** The *what is moving* section of each card is the part to check first.
