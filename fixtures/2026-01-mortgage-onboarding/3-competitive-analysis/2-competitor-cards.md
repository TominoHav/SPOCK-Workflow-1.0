# Competitive Analysis | Competitor Deep-Dive — Mortgage application onboarding

Three participants were profiled and the set is deliberately uneven: a bank, a digital-first bank, and the broker networks that sit between a customer and every lender at once. The brokers are the ones worth watching, because they compete on removing the customer's need to choose a lender rather than on being the lender chosen. None of the three publishes what a first-time applicant is told before they commit to an application.

---

## HypoBank

Researched 2026-08-09 · direct participant · confidence medium [rec:R-A6-01]

### What they offer

End-to-end mortgage with an assisted-digital onboarding

First-time buyers and movers

**No capability was recorded as a separate item.** This card was researched before the standards required five to ten named capabilities, and what the participant does is carried in the paragraphs above rather than enumerated.

### What stands out

**Brand trust with first-time buyers**

**Advisor network that can hand-hold a nervous first purchase**

*Their own positioning.* The safe incumbent — trusted, thorough, slow

### Where the journey stops

Onboarding still requires a branch visit to complete identity

Document upload is email, not in-flow

### What it costs

Rate plus an origination fee, fee waived in branch campaigns

### Confirmed not to do
