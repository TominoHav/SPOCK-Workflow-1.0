## Verdict

Every part below reads elements already in this artifact. **Nothing here introduces a finding the body has
not evidenced** — that is the constraint the verdict runs under, and it is what separates a reading from a
new claim.

### Diagnosis

**This market has digitised the mortgage application and left the applicant alone inside it.** Every
profiled participant offers an end-to-end online journey (B1) and every one of them still answers a stuck
buyer with a branch appointment. The channel moved; the support did not.

### Parity

Save-and-resume across sessions (T1) and phone-camera document capture with instant validation (T3) are
exhibited by every profiled participant. **Omitting either would be a visible deficiency** rather than a
scoping choice, and a rescue flow that reintroduced document friction would read as a regression against
the market's own baseline.

### The opening

**No profiled participant detects a stall and acts on it.** In-flow co-browsing exists in the market and is
not yet common (T2), which is where the emerging tier and the run's absences agree: the mechanism is
available and the application of it to a stalled buyer is unoccupied. Advisers remain the incumbents'
asset (B2) and none has moved that asset into the moment the buyer needs it.

### Risk

**The broker holds this relationship and competes on exactly this ground** (B2). A rescue that routes to a
bank adviser competes with the broker's whole value proposition rather than adding to it, and the market's
commoditised rates (B3) mean there is no price advantage to fall back on if the guidance is worse. The
run's own compliance touchpoints tighten it further: what an adviser says at that hand-off falls inside the
pre-contractual information obligation (R1), so the rescue is a regulated conversation rather than a
helpful one.
