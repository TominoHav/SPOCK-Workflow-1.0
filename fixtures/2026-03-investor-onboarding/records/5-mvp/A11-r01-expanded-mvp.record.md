description: "An explanation indicator is embedded at every question in the suitability questionnaire and at every fund product label in the fund-selection screen. The indicator is visible but occupies minimal layout space; no explanation text is shown until the customer taps or clicks it. On activation the explanation is displayed inline or as an overlay; dismissing it returns the customer to the original screen with questionnaire progress and all prior entries intact. The questionnaire questions, wording, answer options and sequence are unchanged. For questionnaire questions, the explanation states in plain language what the question is asking, why the bank is required to ask it under MiFID II, and what the answer options imply for the customer's investment profile — generically, without referencing this customer's prior answers or profile state. For fund product labels, the explanation defines what the label category means, how a customer's derived risk profile maps to that category, and directs the customer to the applicable Key Information Document for the regulated fund information the label represents. A customer who does not activate any indicator encounters only the unchanged questionnaire and fund-selection experience. The mechanism covers both the suitability questionnaire step and the fund-selection step, so the full scope of ID-2 is piloted in one deployment. The pilot question is whether first-time investors discover and use the explanation mechanism at the moment of uncertainty — whether on-demand access in context at every question and every label is sufficient for the target segment to complete the questionnaire and reach a fund selection without the branch fallback."
