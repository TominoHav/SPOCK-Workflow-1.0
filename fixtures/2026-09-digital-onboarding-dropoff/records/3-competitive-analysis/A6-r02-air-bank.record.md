# Air Bank

**Type:** direct · **Represents:** B5 · **Standing:** leader · **Confidence:** high (6 independent
sources, primary present)

## What they offer

Air Bank is the Czech market's original branchless, fully-digital retail challenger, established in 2011,
now part of the PPF group. [ref:ab-s1] [ref:ab-s4]

- **Account opening in the My Air mobile app, described by the bank as taking "a few taps."** The bank's
  own site states usability begins the moment the account is opened -- "sotva si účet založíte, už ho
  můžete používat" (as soon as you open the account, you can already use it). [ref:ab-s1]
- **Three parallel opening routes -- app, a short online form, or a branch visit** -- all free, with the
  app positioned as the fastest. [ref:ab-s1]
- **A free current account with two cards included and no maintenance fee**, positioned as the base
  product every other feature builds on. [ref:ab-s1]
- **BankID-based identity verification.** Air Bank is a founding member of the Czech BankID consortium.
  [ref:ab-s1]
- **A loyalty-reward programme paying back a percentage on everyday purchases**, automatically applied in
  the app rather than requiring registration per merchant. [ref:ab-s1]
- **Consumer loans and mortgages originated through the same app**, including a "Chytrá rezerva" (smart
  reserve) feature that nets idle savings against mortgage principal to cut interest. [ref:ab-s1]
- **A virtual assistant ("Aneta") inside the app for self-service queries.** [ref:ab-s1]
- **Investment products reachable from the same account**, from CZK 100 monthly contributions to lump-sum
  investing. [ref:ab-s1]

## What stands out

- **"I banku můžete mít rádi"** (you can love this bank too) -- Air Bank's own positioning is explicitly
  about a friendlier banking experience rather than a feature list, and TOP.CZ's independent review
  credits its My Air app with repeated wins in the "Zlatá koruna" mobile-banking-app award category.
  [ref:ab-s1] [ref:ab-s2]
- **Usability rated among the best in the market by independent reviewers.** TradeCZ's review states the
  app is "hodnocena jako jedna z nejlepších na trhu" (rated as one of the best in the market) citing over
  100 configurable parameters and strong security practices. [ref:ab-s4]
- **Fast, low-friction lending decisions.** A reader comment on iDNES.cz's coverage of the bank's growth
  describes being approved for a loan while still in a probationary work period, "vyrizeno za tri dny s
  jedinou navstevou pobocky" (settled in three days with a single branch visit). [ref:ab-s6]

## Where the journey stops

Independent coverage is largely favourable on the account-opening experience specifically, but not
uniformly so on servicing afterward. A Reddit r/czech thread on the bank describes fee-related
frustration at account closure comparable to a rival bank's ("KB stejný problém, poplatky za vedení účtu,
u rušení šílenost" -- same problem as KB, account-maintenance fees, closure is a mess), suggesting the
friction this market shows up downstream of opening rather than during it. [ref:ab-s5] No independent
source consulted for this card describes friction at the identity-verification or account-opening step
itself. [ref:ab-s2] [ref:ab-s3] [ref:ab-s4] [ref:ab-s6]

## What it costs

The current account itself is free with no maintenance fee and two cards included; a CZK 35 fee applies
per cash withdrawal beyond the included allowance, and mortgage rates were quoted by an independent
reviewer at roughly 4.39-4.89% depending on the fixation period in 2026. [ref:ab-s1] [ref:ab-s2]

## Confirmed not to do

Neither the bank's own site nor the five independent reviews consulted describe a save-and-resume
indicator for an application abandoned partway, nor a published maximum time-to-open figure beyond "a
few taps" and "a few clicks" as marketing language. That is what was looked at rather than an inference
from silence. [ref:ab-s1] [ref:ab-s2] [ref:ab-s3] [ref:ab-s4] [ref:ab-s5] [ref:ab-s6]
