## Komerční banka

**Type:** direct · **Represents:** B5, R4 · **Standing:** leader · **Confidence:** high (6 independent
sources, primary present)

### What they offer

Komerční banka is one of the largest full-service retail banks in scope, serving retail, private and
corporate clients from one group. [ref:kb-s1] [ref:kb-s2]

**Free digital current-account tariffs opened remotely.** The bank's own site promotes a "Stát se
klientem" (become a client) flow and time-limited free "Standard" and "Komfort" tariffs for new
clients. [ref:kb-s1]

**KB+, a consolidated mobile and web banking app.** Replacing the bank's older electronic-banking
system, KB+ is marketed as managing "od běžného účtu přes půjčku až k investicím" (from a current
account through a loan to investments) from one app. [ref:kb-s1]

**BankID-based identity verification.** Komerční banka is a founding member of the Czech BankID
consortium, letting a customer of one member bank prove their identity to another using
bank-authenticated credentials rather than a fresh identity check each time. [ref:kb-s1]

**KB Klíč as an authentication and signing tool.** Used across the bank's own app ecosystem for login
and operation authorisation. [ref:kb-s1]

**A bonus-rate savings account bundled with the current-account tariff.** Promoted on the bank's
homepage alongside the tariff sign-up flow. [ref:kb-s1]

**Consumer lending and mortgage origination through the same digital channel**, including an unsecured
"sustainable housing" loan promoted directly from the client homepage. [ref:kb-s1]

**A linked children's account with a wearable payment card**, promoted as a bonus for existing clients'
children rather than a standalone product. [ref:kb-s1]

### What stands out

**"Nová éra bankovnictví je bezpečnější."** The bank's own marketing frames security as the point of
difference for its "Start" tariff aimed at new clients, rather than speed or price. [ref:kb-s1]

**Long-term stability across a wide product portfolio.** Finex.cz's independent review describes
Komerční banka as "dlouhodobě stabilní banka" spanning private and retail banking, rather than a
narrow challenger proposition. [ref:kb-s2]

**In-house access to private-market investing.** The bank's own site promotes "Amundi PRIMA" from
CZK 50,000, positioned as a differentiator against banks without an in-house asset-management
relationship. [ref:kb-s1]

### Where the journey stops

Independent Czech coverage describes friction after account opening rather than at it. On the
modrykonik.cz personal-finance forum, users report that KB's mobile and web banking interface is "méně
přehledné" (less clear) than Air Bank's or MONETA's. [ref:kb-s4] Ekonomický deník and Seznam Zprávy both
cover the bank's 2026 forced migration to the KB+ app: a share of existing clients describe the new
version as "nestabilní a neustále zamrzá" (unstable and keeps freezing), the change was made without a
choice to stay on the old system, and the bank's own spokesperson confirmed the older electronic-banking
platform would be switched off regardless of the complaints. [ref:kb-s5] [ref:kb-s6] Recenzer.cz's
independent review frames the bank as built to serve "sto zákazníků se sto požadavky" (a hundred
customers with a hundred different needs) -- broad rather than focused, which several reviewers connect
to a client area that reads as more complex than a narrower challenger's. [ref:kb-s3]

### What it costs

The bank's own site shows the "Standard" and "Komfort" tariffs free for the first 30 days, with a
time-limited CZK 4,000 new-client bonus advertised on the homepage. The ongoing monthly price of each
tariff after the trial period was not shown on the pages reached in this pass; that is recorded as a gap
below rather than estimated. [ref:kb-s1]

### Confirmed not to do

Neither the bank's own site nor any of the five independent reviews consulted for this card describes a
save-and-resume indicator for an in-progress application, or a stated maximum time-to-open figure. All
five independent sources discuss servicing an existing account rather than a step-by-step account-opening
flow, which is what was looked at rather than treated as evidence of absence. [ref:kb-s1] [ref:kb-s2]
