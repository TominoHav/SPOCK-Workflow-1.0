## Fio banka

**Type:** direct · **Represents:** R4 · **Standing:** leader · **Confidence:** high (6 independent
sources, primary present)

### What they offer

Fio banka is a Czech bank built around a no-fee current account and a self-directed brokerage, run
alongside a mutual-fund business. [ref:fio-s1] [ref:fio-s2]

**A fee-free current account, opened online.** The bank's own site offers "Založit účet online" as a
direct call to action from the homepage, alongside a business-account equivalent. [ref:fio-s1]

**BankID-based identity verification.** Fio banka is a founding member of the Czech BankID consortium.
[ref:fio-s1]

**Integrated share trading and investing from the same account**, through the bank's own "e-Broker"
platform with a demo mode for new users to try before funding it. [ref:fio-s1]

**Fio Smartbanking, the bank's mobile app**, alongside separate internet-banking and e-Broker web
logins. [ref:fio-s1]

**Fee-free mortgages**, promoted on the homepage alongside an overdraft product tied to the current
account. [ref:fio-s1]

**In-house investment research and news**, published on the bank's own site as "Investiční
zpravodajství" and press releases, positioning it as a source of market commentary as well as a
bank. [ref:fio-s1]

**Foreign-currency conversion at published daily rates**, shown directly on the homepage rather than
behind a login. [ref:fio-s1]

### What stands out

**Zero fees as the headline claim.** TOP.CZ's independent review lists "Nulové poplatky" as the bank's
first-named advantage. [ref:fio-s2]

**Breadth of investment products for a retail bank.** TOP.CZ and Finex.cz both single out the combination
of a free current account with a full brokerage and mutual funds as unusual among Czech retail banks of
Fio's size. [ref:fio-s2]

**Transparent account structure.** TOP.CZ describes the account as "transparentní" -- a specific,
named claim about how account information is presented rather than a generic quality statement.
[ref:fio-s2]

### Where the journey stops

Independent sources describe two distinct failure patterns, both downstream of account opening rather
than at it. Firmy.cz's aggregated review summary states client dissatisfaction centres on "problémy s
dostupností podpory, zdlouhavé řešení požadavků" (support availability and slow resolution of requests).
[ref:fio-s3] Separately, a Mapy.com reviewer and a Kurzy.cz forum thread both describe internet-banking
access being restricted or an account being blocked following a security review, with the Kurzy.cz thread
specifically warning about unauthorised-transfer risk on the internet-banking channel if a client is not
careful. [ref:fio-s4] [ref:fio-s5] No independent source consulted for this card describes friction at
account opening or identity verification itself; the sources describe ongoing servicing and security
incidents rather than the opening flow. [ref:fio-s6]

### What it costs

The current account carries no monthly fee per the bank's own site and TOP.CZ's independent confirmation;
incoming international transfers are free per a client comment cited on Mesec.cz. Investment-product
pricing (brokerage commissions, fund fees) is published on the bank's own site under its own pricing pages
but was not itemised within this pass. [ref:fio-s1] [ref:fio-s2] [ref:fio-s6]

### Confirmed not to do

None of the six sources consulted for this card describes a save-and-resume capability for a partially
completed application, or a stated time-to-open figure. That is what was looked at, not an inference from
silence. [ref:fio-s1] [ref:fio-s2] [ref:fio-s3] [ref:fio-s4] [ref:fio-s5] [ref:fio-s6] [rec:R-A6-03]
