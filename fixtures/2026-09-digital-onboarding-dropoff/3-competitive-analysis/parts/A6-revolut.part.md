## Revolut

**Type:** direct · **Represents:** T1, B1 · **Standing:** leader · **Confidence:** high (6 independent
sources, primary present)

### What they offer

Revolut is a UK-headquartered digital bank operating at global scale, structured around one app spanning
banking, cards, investing and crypto. [ref:rv-s1]

**Sign-up described by the bank as "free in a tap."** The homepage's own framing is a single-step,
in-app registration rather than a multi-page form. [ref:rv-s1]

**A tiered plan ladder (Standard, Plus, Premium, Metal, Ultra)** with published monthly prices from free
up to GBP 55/month, each unlocking travel, insurance and lounge-access benefits. [ref:rv-s1]

**In-app salary switching and automated saving/budgeting tools.** [ref:rv-s1]

**In-app stock and ETF investing**, commission-free within a monthly allowance, run by Revolut's own
regulated trading entity. [ref:rv-s1]

**Instant Access Savings paying up to 4% AER (variable)**, tiered by plan. [ref:rv-s1]

**An AI assistant ("AIR") reachable from inside the app** for account queries and actions. [ref:rv-s1]

**International money transfer to a long list of named corridors** (India, Nigeria, Saudi Arabia and
others), each with its own dedicated page. [ref:rv-s1]

**A 24/7 in-house financial-crime team and two-factor authentication** described as standing security
infrastructure rather than an add-on. [ref:rv-s1]

### What stands out

**Scale as the headline claim.** The bank's own homepage cites 80+ million customers worldwide, 13
million in the UK, and multiple "World's Best Digital Bank" awards. [ref:rv-s1]

**Instant onboarding as a category-defining trait**, per independent fintech commentary: "Neobanks like
Revolut... have reset our expectations for money: instant onboarding, real-time alerts, slick FX." [ref:rv-s5]

**Named as one of the segment's top challengers year over year**, alongside N26, Monzo and bunq, in
independent 2026 fintech-sector coverage. [ref:rv-s5]

### Where the journey stops

Independent review sources are sharply negative about post-opening account access, not about the opening
step itself. Unstar.app's aggregated review analysis states the top two complaint patterns in Revolut's
1-star reviews are an account frozen with the balance locked inside (30%) and support reduced to a
chatbot with no human escalation (24%). [ref:rv-s2] Trustpilot reviews quoted directly describe funds
blocked "without any reason," transfers rejected with only a generic "our security system blocked it"
explanation, and formal complaints taking multiple weeks with no phone line available. [ref:rv-s3] A
Reddit thread offers a counter-read: "Revolut is actually superb if you're not shady... if you do shady
shit like suspicious transfers, you absolutely... will get your account locked," framing the pattern as a
fraud-control side effect rather than a random failure. [ref:rv-s4] No source consulted for this card
describes friction at the identity-verification or account-opening step specifically; every complaint
found concerns access to funds or support after an account is already open. [ref:rv-s5] [ref:rv-s6]

### What it costs

Standard is free; Plus is GBP 3.99/month, Premium GBP 7.99/month, Metal GBP 14.99/month, Ultra GBP
55/month, each with a stated feature set on the bank's own comparison page. Investment trading carries a
0.25% fee outside the monthly allowance for Standard-to-Metal plans, 0.12% for Ultra. [ref:rv-s1]

### Confirmed not to do

Neither Revolut's own site nor the five independent sources consulted describe a save-and-resume
capability for a partially completed application, nor a published guaranteed time-to-open figure beyond
"free in a tap" as marketing language. That is what was looked at, not an inference from silence.
