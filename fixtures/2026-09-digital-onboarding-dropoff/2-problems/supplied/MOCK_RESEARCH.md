# Mock Research: Digital Onboarding Drop-off

> SYNTHETIC RESEARCH FOR MVP TESTING ONLY

1. Participant 1 left after identity verification because the next steps and remaining time were unclear.
2. Participant 2 stopped after a failed document scan and did not understand whether progress had been saved.
3. Participant 3 completed identity creation but believed the current account was already open.
4. Participant 4 switched from mobile to desktop after receiving an error and then restarted the journey.
5. Participant 5 completed the process but described the number of confirmations as repetitive.

## Selected quotations

- “I did not know how many steps were still waiting.”
- “After the scan failed, I was not sure whether I had to start again.”
- “I thought creating the identity meant the account was finished.”

## Research note

The evidence suggests several possible causes rather than one confirmed cause. Some observations concern clarity, while others concern technical recovery or the distinction between identity and product completion.
