# Competitor cards: account switching market participants

The ten cards show a single structural gap common to every participant in this set: card-on-file payments fall outside the scope of both CASS and PAD switching frameworks, and no bank has deployed tooling to identify or migrate them. UK neobanks have converged on salary arrival as the completion signal, treating the first post-switch payday as the moment the product activates; Czech banks operate at the PAD statutory floor with no feature built around that event. Primary sites for three participants were unreachable during research, so the Revolut, CSOB, and Air Bank cards rest on secondary publishers for their account of the switching experience. The self-managed approach is the only method in this set that preserves continuous visibility of the old account throughout the transition, and that visibility is the benchmark every guided service in this set competes against without yet matching it.

## Starling Bank

[rec:A6-r01]

Starling Bank is the UK neobank that has gone furthest toward addressing the card-on-file problem structurally, without fully solving it. Its combination of virtual cards and named Saving Spaces isolates subscription credentials from the main account card, which reduces the manual update burden a CASS switch creates. At 4.6 million accounts with 10% annual growth and no cash switching incentive, the product is growing on the strength of the account itself. Starling makes no product intervention at the salary-arrival moment.

### What they offer

Starling provides a fee-free personal current account with a fully digital CASS switching journey to UK residents aged 18 and over. It holds a full UK banking licence, is regulated by the PRA and FCA, and offers FSCS deposit protection up to £85,000.

**Starling runs a full CASS switch inside its mobile app, with the old account closing automatically on switch day.**
The customer enters their old bank's sort code and account number; Starling handles the CASS mechanics from there. The switch completes within 7 working days and is all-or-nothing: no partial transfer option is available. Incoming payments redirect from the old account to the new one for a minimum of three years under CASS rules.

**Up to five virtual cards, each linked to a named Saving Space, let customers assign specific categories of spending to a separate card number.**
Each virtual card is free to create and functions as a standard Mastercard debit card. A customer who routes subscriptions through a dedicated Subscriptions Space and virtual card sees all recurring charges in one place, and those merchant relationships are attached to the virtual card number rather than the main account card. The virtual card works independently of the main account card, so changes to one do not affect the other.

**Saving Spaces divide the account balance into named sub-accounts without requiring a separate sort code or account number.**
Each Space earns up to 4.50% AER interest on the balance held within it. Spaces serve both savings goals and spending categories. The virtual card feature makes any Space into a funding source for a distinct card number, combining budgeting and payment separation in one structure.

**The personal current account charges no monthly fee, no UK ATM fee, no overseas ATM fee and no foreign transaction fee.**
Faster Payments within the UK are free. Cash deposits at Post Office branches are free up to £10,000 per day with immediate clearing. Card replacements, whether standard or lost or stolen, carry no charge. There is no minimum deposit and no minimum balance requirement.

**An arranged overdraft is available at 15% EAR, charged daily and billed monthly.**
No banding applies; one rate covers all eligible customers. Transactions that would exceed the arranged limit are declined rather than incurring an unarranged overdraft charge. The specific rate and the absence of tiering are both stated publicly on the fees page.

**Spending Intelligence, an AI-powered tool launched in January 2026, analyses transaction patterns and surfaces categorised spending insights inside the app.**
The tool extends Starling's existing transaction categorisation, adding pattern detection and natural-language queries over spending history. A gamified balance-awareness feature called Last Digit Widget was released at the same time as part of a broader money management suite.

**24/7 UK-based phone support is available to all personal current account holders.**
Monzo and Revolut handle most customer contact through in-app chat. Starling maintains a phone channel around the clock for all account types. Three independent review sources covering 2025-2026 rank Starling as the most phone-accessible of the three major UK neobanks.

**FSCS deposit protection covers balances up to £85,000 per person.**
Starling holds a full UK banking licence and is regulated by the PRA and the FCA. Deposit protection places Starling on the same regulatory footing as Lloyds Bank and NatWest within this profile set, and equal to Monzo and Revolut.

### What stands out

**Virtual cards linked to Saving Spaces create a structural path to reducing card-on-file credential exposure during a CASS switch, and no other participant in this profile set offers the same combination.**
A customer who assigns subscriptions to a dedicated virtual card before switching retains those merchant relationships on an instrument that is independent of the main account card. When the CASS switch changes the main card number, the virtual card number is unaffected, and the merchants attached to it require no credential update. No other bank in this set combines a digital-first switching journey with a pre-switch subscription isolation tool. The feature requires deliberate setup by the customer and does not migrate existing merchant relationships. Sources: `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`, `S-moneytothemasses-starling-bank-current-account-r`.

**Starling has grown to 4.6 million personal accounts at 10% annual growth without offering cash switching incentives.**
Its August-October 2026 switch offer gives new customers completing a CASS transfer within 30 days a SURI Pro 2.0 electric toothbrush, with a retail value of £105, rather than cash. Monzo and NatWest use cash offers in the same period. Growth at this rate without cash payments is the strongest available indicator that the product itself drives switching, rather than incentive spend. Source: `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`, `S-en-starling-bank-article`.

**The arranged overdraft rate of 15% EAR is the lowest among the three UK neobanks profiled in this set.**
Monzo's arranged overdraft runs from 19% to 39% EAR depending on the customer. NatWest charges 39.49% EAR. Starling applies one rate to all eligible customers without banding, making overdraft pricing transparent and predictable. Source: `S-pocketwise-starling-bank-fees-uk-2026`.

**24/7 UK phone support distinguishes Starling from Monzo and Revolut within the UK neobank set.**
Three independent review sources covering 2025-2026 rank Starling as the most accessible of the three for human contact. The phone channel is available regardless of account type or issue, and is described by Good Money Guide reviewers as the most responsive of the three. Sources: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`, `S-moneytothemasses-starling-bank-current-account-r`, `S-moneyweek-starling-bank-review-and-account-compa`.

### Where the journey stops

Card-on-file subscriptions are outside CASS scope and require manual credential updating after the switch. Starling's own switching documentation confirms this: customers must update card details stored by merchants in the same way as they would if their card expired. The virtual card feature reduces this problem for customers who set it up before switching. It does nothing for customers who did not, and does nothing for existing merchant relationships already attached to the main card.

Trustpilot records over 46,000 customer reviews for Starling, and three recurring failure patterns appear across them: accounts closed without advance notice or explanation, transactions blocked without an immediate customer verification opportunity, and customer service calls that fail to resolve issues. One reviewer describes a 32-minute call with no outcome. Source: `S-trustpilot-starling-bank-customer-reviews`.

Good Money Guide reviewers note that customers held in Starling's account review process can be blocked from accessing their own funds for multiple days, with information requested sequentially rather than in a single step. Source: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`.

The FCA fined Starling £29 million in October 2024 for anti-money laundering control failures, described in the FCA's public statement as "shockingly lax." The bank paid the fine and remains operational. The heightened screening that the AML remediation introduced provides the context for the elevated rate of account restrictions and transaction blocks visible in customer complaints. Source: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`.

Starling removed interest from the main personal current account balance in February 2025. Interest is now only available within Saving Spaces. Customers who hold a working balance in the main account earn nothing on it. Source: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`.

No physical branches. Cash deposit options are limited to Post Office branches rather than ATMs or retail locations. Source: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`.

### What it costs

Personal Current Account: no monthly fee, no minimum deposit, no minimum balance. UK ATM withdrawals are unlimited and free. Overseas ATM withdrawals are unlimited and free worldwide, with no fair-use cap or daily limit beyond the account balance. Foreign card payments carry no transaction fee, using the Mastercard network rate. Faster Payments transfers within the UK are free. Cash deposits at Post Office branches are free up to £10,000 per day. Standard and lost-or-stolen card replacements are free.

Arranged overdraft: 15% EAR, charged daily and billed monthly. Unarranged overdraft: not offered. Transactions beyond the arranged limit are declined.

International transfers: a conversion fee is built into the displayed exchange rate. The specific percentage is not published on the public fees page; the fee is shown in the transfer flow before the customer confirms.

Business Current Account: £7 per month. EUR business account: £2 per month as an add-on. Bulk Payments add-on: £7 per month.

Source: `S-pocketwise-starling-bank-fees-uk-2026`.

### Confirmed not to do

Card-on-file migration at the point of switch is not offered. Starling's own switching documentation states that customers must update card details stored by merchants manually after the switch completes. The virtual card feature addresses pre-switch setup only; it does not contact merchants on the customer's behalf. Source: `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`.

Salary sorting is not available. Monzo's Salary Sorter divides incoming salary automatically across named Pots on arrival, and Monzo's own product page documents this feature. Starling's features pages list no equivalent. Saving Spaces can be funded manually but do not respond automatically to salary arrival. Sources: `S-monzo-cass-switching-page-get-paid-early-page-sa`, `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`.

Early salary release is not offered. Monzo's Get Paid Early releases salary 24 hours before standard clearing, and Revolut's Early Salary operates the same mechanism. Starling's product pages list no equivalent feature. Sources: `S-monzo-cass-switching-page-get-paid-early-page-sa`, `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`.

Employer salary notification is not part of the switching journey. Starling's switching page confirms that updating salary payment instructions with the employer is the customer's responsibility. Source: `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`.

## Monzo

[rec:A6-r02]

Monzo is a UK retail bank licensed by the PRA and FCA, with FSCS-protected deposits and a fully app-based current account. Its switching proposition is built around the salary-arrival moment as the point at which a switch is understood to be complete. Salary Sorter and Get Paid Early turn the first post-switch payday from an unremarkable deposit into a product interaction the customer has set up in advance.

### What they offer

Monzo provides current accounts for individuals aged 16 and over in the UK, with a free base tier and three paid plans. It operates through a smartphone app with no branch network, serving a broad retail segment.

**Monzo runs a full CASS switch from within its app, completing in seven working days.**

The customer enters their old bank's sort code and account number, and Monzo coordinates the transfer of direct debits, standing orders and account balance. The old bank notifies all direct debit originators. Incoming payments to the old account are redirected automatically for at least three years. The old account closes on the completion date, and the switch date can be set in advance. Address details must match between the old bank and Monzo before the switch can start.

**Salary Sorter distributes an incoming salary across named Pots the moment it arrives.**

Any incoming payment over GBP 100 triggers Salary Sorter on the transaction notification, presenting a split screen where the customer allocates percentages or fixed amounts to existing Pots. The customer confirms the split, and Monzo distributes the funds at once. The preference is saved and applied automatically to the next payment from the same sender. Pots must be created before the first salary lands for the feature to work.

**Get Paid Early releases salary up to 24 hours before the standard clearing date.**

When a salary payment appears in the clearing window, Monzo credits the funds ahead of the standard settlement time. The feature activates once a recurring salary deposit is detected from the same sender. Combined with Salary Sorter, this means the first post-switch payday arrives early and is immediately allocated as the customer previously set up.

**Pots are named sub-accounts within the main balance, used to separate bills, savings and spending.**

Customers create Pots for specific purposes: a bills Pot that pays direct debits, a savings Pot for a goal, a spending Pot for discretionary money. Standing orders and direct debits can be configured to draw from a specific Pot rather than the main balance. The customer who sets up their Pots before the switch completes has their money structure ready for the first salary arrival. Pots are the prerequisite for Salary Sorter.

**Virtual cards are available on paid plans and let customers assign a separate card number to specific subscriptions.**

A virtual card is a distinct card number that can be linked to a particular merchant or subscription. If that virtual card is replaced, only the subscriptions routed to it require updating. The main physical card number stays intact. This does not eliminate the card-on-file problem at the point of switching, but it reduces its scale for customers who use virtual cards for recurring charges before a switch. Available from the Extra plan at GBP 3 per month.

**FSCS protection covers deposits up to GBP 120,000 across all plan tiers, including the free account.**

The GBP 120,000 ceiling is higher than the standard GBP 85,000 that applies to most UK banks in this set. It is available on the free account, not from a paid tier. This places Monzo on clearer footing than Revolut's pre-2026 e-money model, and it is a fact the switching proposition can state plainly.

**An arranged overdraft is available to eligible customers, with the daily cost shown in pounds before the customer accepts.**

Monzo's in-app overdraft approval process shows the daily cost in pounds and pence rather than an annual rate, reducing the cognitive load on a customer comparing options. No unarranged overdraft fee applies. The app signals balance position in real time and notifies the customer when they approach a negative balance.

**Four subscription tiers offer escalating benefits from a free account to a full insurance bundle.**

Free (GBP 0/month): UK current account with FSCS protection to GBP 120,000. Extra (GBP 3/month): adds virtual cards, 3.00% AER Instant Access savings and unlimited connected external accounts. Perks (GBP 7/month for customers who joined before 13 August 2026, GBP 9/month for all from 14 October 2026): adds 3.25% AER Instant Access savings, an annual Railcard, weekly Greggs snacks, monthly Vue cinema tickets, Uber One and YouTube Premium Lite. Max (GBP 17/month for pre-August customers, GBP 19/month for all from October 2026): adds worldwide travel insurance, phone insurance and UK and Europe breakdown cover, with the option to add up to five family members at GBP 4/month each.

**Monzo publishes its net CASS gain figure each quarter, treating account switching as a disclosed growth metric.**

Q3 2025: 9,934 net CASS gains. No other neobank in this set publishes an equivalent number. Disclosing it means switching volume is an auditable figure rather than an internal performance measure, and it confirms that CASS switching is a managed acquisition channel, not a compliance obligation.

**Monzo launched an eSIM mobile service in 2026 through a partnership with VMO2 and 1Global, adding a phone bill to the banking app.**

Monzo Mobile plans start at GBP 8 per month. A loyalty discount reduces the monthly cost by 5% each year for up to six years, giving a maximum reduction of 30% over that period. The service sits inside the existing Monzo app. It is the first time Monzo has extended into a recurring service category outside banking.

### What stands out

**Monzo has built two features that only activate when salary arrives, making the first post-switch payday the product completion event.**

Salary Sorter and Get Paid Early are not general budgeting tools attached to a switching service. They are specifically triggered by salary receipt, and they are the reason a customer would switch to Monzo rather than simply open a second account elsewhere. No other participant in this set treats salary arrival as the signal that the switch is working. The product sequence, switch to activate both features and then receive salary, is a deliberate design choice. Source: monzo.com CASS switching and Salary Sorter help pages.

**9,934 net CASS gains in Q3 2025 is a publicly auditable figure; no other UK neobank in this set discloses one.**

CASS publishes per-institution net switching data, and Monzo voluntarily named its own figure. That makes Monzo's switching trajectory the only one in the challenger segment that an external analyst can track across quarters. The Q3 2025 number places Monzo among the top positive performers in the full CASS participant set, a list that includes over fifty institutions. Source: currentaccountswitch.co.uk, Q3 2025 net switching data.

**Monzo rated 4.84 out of 5 from 19,427 reviews on Smart Money People, with 96% five-star ratings.**

Smart Money People is a UK financial product review platform independent of Monzo. 19,427 submitted reviews is a volume large enough to carry structural weight, and 96% at five stars is not a marginal result. The score confirms that the day-to-day product experience matches the product's positioning, rather than existing only in marketing copy. Source: smartmoneypeople.com, Monzo current account reviews.

**Monzo reported sustained profitability in FY26 alongside continued customer growth, confirming that the free-tier acquisition model and paid-tier revenue mix have reached a viable unit economics position.**

FinTech Magazine reported in 2026 that Monzo's FY26 results combined rapid customer growth with sustained profits and a more diversified revenue model. A profitable neobank with continued growth invests in product from its own cash flow rather than from external capital, which changes the pace at which it can ship and the scope of what it bets on. Source: fintechmagazine.com, FY26 results article.

### Where the journey stops

Card-on-file payments, subscriptions and recurring charges stored against a card number at the merchant, fall outside CASS scope and Monzo provides no separate mechanism to identify or migrate them. After the switch closes the old account, there is no Monzo feature that shows what payments are still pointing at the former bank. This is confirmed by CASS scope documentation and by Smart Money People reviews, none of which describe any post-switch residual payment visibility tool. Account freezing during automated compliance checks is the most documented serious complaint in independent reviews: accounts can be suspended for multiple days with funds inaccessible and no stated timeline for resolution, as recorded on Smart Money People. Monzo provides no telephone support channel; all customer contact is through in-app chat, which reviewers cite as a difficulty when an account is frozen and the queue is long.

### What it costs

Four plan tiers. Free (GBP 0/month): UK current account with FSCS protection to GBP 120,000 and basic savings. Extra (GBP 3/month): virtual cards, 3.00% AER Instant Access savings, unlimited linked external accounts. Perks (GBP 7/month for customers who joined before 13 August 2026, rising to GBP 9/month for all customers from 14 October 2026): 3.25% AER Instant Access savings, annual Railcard, weekly Greggs snacks, monthly Vue cinema ticket, Uber One, YouTube Premium Lite. Max (GBP 17/month for pre-August customers, rising to GBP 19/month for all from 14 October 2026): all Perks benefits plus worldwide travel insurance, phone insurance and UK and Europe breakdown cover, with up to five family members added at GBP 4/month each. Legacy Plus and Premium plans move to GBP 6 and GBP 17/month respectively from October 2026. Monzo Mobile eSIM plans start separately at GBP 8/month with a loyalty discount.

### Confirmed not to do

Card-on-file payment migration at switch: CASS covers direct debits and standing orders only, and Monzo's own CASS help page describes no mechanism for card-stored merchant credentials. Looked at: monzo.com help page on understanding CASS, which lists what the switch covers without mentioning card-on-file payments, confirming the omission is structural rather than a documentation gap. Post-switch residual payment view: no Monzo help page and no independent review of its switching journey describes a dashboard or notification system showing what is still running on the closed old account. Looked at: monzo.com switching help pages, Smart Money People reviews. Telephone customer support: Monzo's contact model is in-app chat only, confirmed in Smart Money People and Finder reviews and not contested by any Monzo-published material.

## Revolut

[rec:A6-r03]

Revolut is a UK financial technology company that operated as a licensed e-money institution from its founding in 2015 until March 2026, when the Prudential Regulation Authority and Financial Conduct Authority granted it a full UK banking licence. That licensing change delivers two consequences directly relevant to account switching: FSCS deposit protection now applies to UK current accounts, and Revolut joined the Current Account Switch Service as a full member. Approximately 13 million UK customers are being migrated from e-money accounts to current accounts in tranches since March 2026. Its switching proposition centres on Early Salary, which credits incoming salary 24 hours before standard Faster Payments clearing, and on multi-currency accounts covering 36 currencies at weekday interbank rate.

### What they offer

Revolut provides personal current accounts, multi-currency banking and financial services to UK retail customers across a five-tier plan structure from Standard (no monthly fee) to Ultra.

**Revolut participates in CASS as a licensed bank from March 2026, offering transfer of balance, direct debits and standing orders within 7 working days, backed by the Current Account Switch Guarantee.**
Customers may opt for a full switch, in which the old account closes on completion, or a partial switch, in which payments redirect while the old account remains open. Incoming payment redirection from the old account continues for at least 3 years as part of the standard CASS service. CASS participation is a direct consequence of the banking licence; Revolut could not join CASS while operating as an e-money institution.

**Early Salary credits incoming salary to the Revolut account 24 hours before standard Faster Payments clearing, activating automatically once a recurring employer payment is detected.**
For a customer switching via CASS, Early Salary fires on the first salary received in the new account, shortening the perceived gap between completing the switch and normal financial operation. The mechanism is the same as Monzo's Get Paid Early. No manual activation beyond completing the CASS switch is required.

**Multi-currency accounts hold balances in 36 currencies simultaneously, with weekday exchange executed at the interbank mid-market rate and no markup.**
Weekend exchange incurs a surcharge of approximately 1 percent. Spending via the Revolut Visa debit card is available in 150 currencies. The Standard tier includes GBP 1,000 per month of fee-free weekday currency exchange. This differentiates Revolut from Monzo, Starling, and every Czech and UK incumbent in this set for internationally mobile customers.

**Virtual cards can be created and deleted in-app, allowing customers to route subscription and recurring merchant payments to a card number separate from their main Visa debit card.**
A customer who has directed recurring subscriptions to a virtual card rather than the main account card has fewer merchant credentials to update when switching banks. Virtual cards can be frozen or cancelled independently of the primary card. This is a partial structural mitigation for the card-on-file problem that CASS does not address.

**FSCS deposit protection up to GBP 85,000 per person applies to all Revolut Bank UK Ltd current accounts, replacing the safeguarding structure in use when Revolut operated as an e-money institution.**

## Self-managed switch (no guided service)

[rec:A6-r08]

A self-managed switch is what a customer does when they move their main banking relationship without using any bank's guided switching service. The customer keeps both accounts open, monitors the old account for payments that continue to arrive, and redirects each one individually to the new account. No scheme participation is required and no switch date is imposed. This approach is the baseline the opportunities in this run are measuring against: it is what a customer does when no guided service exists, or when they do not trust a guided service to handle the move completely.

### What they offer

This approach covers any bank account move where the customer is the sole manager of the transition. It is used across all markets that lack a formal switching service, and in markets that have one by customers who prefer control over convenience.

**The customer can move to any bank, regardless of scheme membership.** Automated switching services such as CASS require both the old and new bank to participate in the scheme. A self-managed switch has no such requirement. A customer moving to a newly licensed bank, a fintech account, an institution outside the switching scheme, or a bank in a different regulatory market can only complete the move this way. MoneySavingExpert and MoneySuperMarket both confirm that non-scheme participants cannot offer the automated alternative.

**The old account remains open and visible for the full duration of the move.** Every payment still pointing at the old account is visible to the customer in real time. This is the structural feature that distinguishes self-managed switching from any bank-managed service: no guided service offers a view of what is still running on the old account after it has been closed, because the old account is closed as part of the guided service. Which? and GoCompare confirm that customers choose self-managed switching specifically to retain this visibility before committing to closure.

**The customer sets their own timeline with no imposed switch date.** Bank-managed switching services set a completion date from which a countdown begins. A self-managed switch runs for as long as the customer needs. Consumer guidance from Which?, MoneySavingExpert and Money to the Masses describes a typical active management period of 30 to 90 days, with no contractual end point.

**Each payment is verified individually before the next is moved.** The customer does not move all payments on a single date. Salary can be confirmed as arriving at the new account before any direct debits are changed, and each direct debit can be confirmed before the next is moved. This produces a payment-by-payment record of what has moved and what has not, which no switching scheme generates for the customer.

**The old account closes only when the customer judges the move complete.** There is no automatic closure instruction and no bank-initiated account shutdown. The customer closes the old account themselves at a time of their choosing, after satisfying themselves that nothing is still arriving there. This eliminates the risk that a bank-managed switch closes the account before an undetected payment has been redirected.

**The customer controls the order in which payments are moved.** Critical obligations, salary and rent, can be moved and confirmed before lower-priority subscriptions. A CASS switch moves all qualifying payment types on a single switch date with no customer-set priority ordering.

**The switch handles card-on-file subscriptions with the same method it uses for every other payment.** Card-on-file subscriptions, invoice payment authorities, and merchant-stored card credentials must be updated manually under any approach: CASS does not transfer them. A self-managed switch does not treat these as residual work left over from an automated process. The customer contacts each merchant directly in the same way they contact each direct debit originator, using one method for everything.

**The customer retains full access to the old account's transaction history for as long as it is kept open.** Banks are obliged to provide statements on request. A self-managed switcher who keeps the old account active has continuous access to the payment record that shows what is still running there, for as long as the account remains open.

### What stands out

**The live view of old-account activity is the one capability no bank switching service can match.** When a guided service closes the old account as part of the switch, the customer loses sight of any payment still pointing there. A self-managed switcher who keeps the old account open sees every missed payment before it becomes a problem. Which? and MoneySavingExpert confirm that the desire to maintain this visibility is the primary reason customers choose this approach over an automated service. No participant in the direct competitor set provides an equivalent post-switch view once the old account is closed.

**Consumer guidance converges on "considerable attention to detail" as the defining cost of this approach.** Which?, MoneySavingExpert, Money to the Masses and Citizens Advice all describe self-managed switching in these terms or close equivalents. The phrase is the market's collective assessment of what the approach costs, and it is the standard against which every bank switching service positions itself. When a bank claims its service reduces friction, it is measuring that friction against this baseline.

**A substantial proportion of self-managed switch attempts do not reach a defined completion.** GoCompare and Money to the Masses both describe a pattern in which customers who begin a self-managed switch end up running two accounts indefinitely because they are never confident that all payments have moved. The switch becomes a state of permanent dual-account maintenance rather than a completed transition. This is the friction floor that O1 and O3 in this run are designed to close.

**The card-on-file problem is structurally harder in a self-managed switch than in any scheme-based alternative.** Under CASS, direct debits and standing orders transfer automatically, leaving card-on-file subscriptions as the residual the customer must handle. Under a self-managed switch, nothing transfers automatically. The customer must move direct debits, standing orders, salary notifications, and card-on-file subscriptions individually. MoneySuperMarket confirms that recurring card payments require manual updating regardless of which approach is used; the self-managed route carries the full direct debit and standing order burden on top of that shared card-on-file problem.

### Where the journey stops

The process has no defined end state. There is no external authority, no switching service, and no bank that can confirm to the customer that the move is complete. The decision to close the old account is a judgment the customer makes without any systematic check. Which? and MoneySavingExpert acknowledge this directly: the customer's confidence rests on a period of observation during which no unexpected payments arrived at the old account, and the customer chooses how long that period must be.

No tool exists to generate a list of card-on-file merchants. The customer must construct the list from memory, from a review of old statements, or by waiting for a payment to fail. Citizens Advice and MoneySavingExpert both identify this task as the most consistently underestimated part of the process. A subscription the customer did not recall will continue charging the old card until the charge is declined, at which point the merchant may cancel the subscription before the customer has redirected it.

Salary notification is fully manual and cannot be confirmed until the first salary payment completes at the new account. If the employer's payroll cycle is monthly, the customer may wait up to four weeks to discover that the notification was not processed. This single unconfirmable step is the one that bears most directly on O2 in this run.

The continued operation of the old account creates an overdraft risk. If the customer reduces the old account balance before all incoming payments have been redirected, a payment that arrives after the drawdown may trigger an unarranged overdraft and the associated fees. GoCompare and MoneySavingExpert both identify this as the main direct financial risk of running the two accounts simultaneously.

There is no financial guarantee. The CASS guarantee covers customers when a bank-managed switch results in a missed payment or an incorrect charge. A self-managed switch carries no equivalent protection. The Current Account Switch Service confirms that the guarantee applies only to switches conducted through the scheme.

### What it costs

The switch itself costs nothing to initiate. The costs that accumulate during a self-managed transition are indirect.

Monthly fees on the old account continue for the full duration of the move. Many standard current accounts are free to hold. Accounts with packaged benefits carry monthly charges: NatWest Flex at £2 per month, Lloyds Club at £3 per month and Monzo Max at £17 per month are the published rates for participants in this set. A 90-day transition on a £3-per-month account adds £9.

Overdraft fees apply if a payment arrives at the old account after the balance has been reduced. MoneySavingExpert and GoCompare both flag this as the main direct financial risk.

Time is the largest cost. Which? and Money to the Masses estimate two to four hours of active work to construct the payment list and contact each payee. Employers and utilities may take two to four weeks to process a change of bank account details. The monitoring period, during which the customer must check the old account regularly for payments still arriving, typically runs for 30 to 90 days.

No cash switching incentive applies. Banks that run CASS-linked incentive programmes, including Lloyds at £200 to £500 and NatWest with seasonal cash offers, tie those incentives to customers who switch through the CASS service. A self-managed move to any bank, including banks running active incentive programmes, does not qualify.

### Confirmed not to do

Direct debits do not transfer automatically. CASS transfers direct debits and standing orders as its core function. A self-managed switch requires the customer to cancel each direct debit at the old bank and set up each new one individually at the new bank. Which?, Citizens Advice and MoneySavingExpert all confirm this. The Current Account Switch Service documentation confirms that direct debit transfer is a capability of the scheme, not of any approach that works outside it.

Incoming payment redirection is not provided. CASS redirects incoming payments from the old account to the new one for at least three years in the UK following a switch. A self-managed switch has no equivalent mechanism. Any payment that arrives at the old account after the customer has reduced the balance stays there unless the payer has already been notified. The Current Account Switch Service confirms that incoming payment redirection is a scheme feature only.

No financial guarantee covers the move. The Current Account Switch Service states that its guarantee applies only to switches conducted through the service. A self-managed switch carries no indemnity against a failed payment or a missed charge.

A list of card-on-file merchants cannot be generated by the customer, any bank, or any switching service in this market. Card credentials are stored by merchants against a card number, not by banks against an account. No participant in this set, and no switching service in any market covered by this research, has the ability to retrieve or surface a list of merchants holding a customer's old card details. MoneySuperMarket and MoneySavingExpert both confirm this. It is a structural limitation shared by all switching approaches, but the self-managed approach carries it alongside the full direct debit task rather than having the direct debit task removed first.

Employer or payroll notification is not sent automatically. Which?, Citizens Advice and every bank switching guide in this set confirm that notifying an employer of a new account number is always the customer's responsibility, under any approach. The self-managed switch differs only in that the customer must also notify all other payees individually rather than having direct debits and standing orders transferred on their behalf.
