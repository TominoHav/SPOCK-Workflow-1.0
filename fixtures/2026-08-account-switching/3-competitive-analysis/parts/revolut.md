## Revolut

[rec:A6-r03]

Revolut is a UK financial technology company that operated as a licensed e-money institution from its founding in 2015 until March 2026, when the Prudential Regulation Authority and Financial Conduct Authority granted it a full UK banking licence. That licensing change delivers two consequences directly relevant to account switching: FSCS deposit protection now applies to UK current accounts, and Revolut joined the Current Account Switch Service as a full member. Approximately 13 million UK customers are being migrated from e-money accounts to current accounts in tranches since March 2026. Its switching proposition centres on Early Salary, which credits incoming salary 24 hours before standard Faster Payments clearing, and on multi-currency accounts covering 36 currencies at weekday interbank rate.

### What they offer

Revolut provides personal current accounts, multi-currency banking and financial services to UK retail customers across a five-tier plan structure from Standard (no monthly fee) to Ultra.

**Revolut participates in CASS as a licensed bank from March 2026, offering transfer of balance, direct debits and standing orders within 7 working days, backed by the Current Account Switch Guarantee.**
Customers may opt for a full switch, in which the old account closes on completion, or a partial switch, in which payments redirect while the old account remains open. Incoming payment redirection from the old account continues for at least 3 years as part of the standard CASS service. CASS participation is a direct consequence of the banking licence; Revolut could not join CASS while operating as an e-money institution.

**Early Salary credits incoming salary to the Revolut account 24 hours before standard Faster Payments clearing, activating automatically once a recurring employer payment is detected.**
For a customer switching via CASS, Early Salary fires on the first salary received in the new account, shortening the perceived gap between completing the switch and normal financial operation. The mechanism is the same as Monzo's Get Paid Early. No manual activation beyond completing the CASS switch is required.

**Multi-currency accounts hold balances in 36 currencies simultaneously, with weekday exchange executed at the interbank mid-market rate and no markup.**
Weekend exchange incurs a surcharge of approximately 1 percent. Spending via the Revolut Visa debit card is available in 150 currencies. The Standard tier includes GBP 1,000 per month of fee-free weekday currency exchange. This differentiates Revolut from Monzo, Starling, and every Czech and UK incumbent in this set for internationally mobile customers.

**Virtual cards can be created and deleted in-app, allowing customers to route subscription and recurring merchant payments to a card number separate from their main Visa debit card.**
A customer who has directed recurring subscriptions to a virtual card rather than the main account card has fewer merchant credentials to update when switching banks. Virtual cards can be frozen or cancelled independently of the primary card. This is a partial structural mitigation for the card-on-file problem that CASS does not address.

**FSCS deposit protection up to GBP 85,000 per person applies to all Revolut Bank UK Ltd current accounts, replacing the safeguarding structure in use when Revolut operated as an e-money institution.**
Temporary high balance protection covers up to GBP 1 million for 6 months following qualifying life events such as a house sale or inheritance. Prior to March 2026, Revolut customer funds were held in ring-fenced safeguarded accounts rather than covered by FSCS; the banking licence closes that gap.

**Savings vaults within the current account earn variable interest rates, with balances remaining instantly accessible and round-up savings from card spending directed automatically to named vaults.**
Customers can create multiple vaults for different savings purposes. Interest rates are variable and increase on higher plan tiers. Vaults operate within the main Revolut app and require no separate savings product application.

**App-only onboarding completes in under three minutes, requiring a photograph of a UK identity document and a selfie video, with no in-branch visit, no minimum income requirement and no minimum opening deposit.**
The account provides a sort code, account number and GB-prefixed IBAN on opening. Apple Pay and Google Pay are available on the same session. Identity verification is automated; complex documents may trigger manual review. No web-only signup option is available.

### What stands out

**The March 2026 UK banking licence transforms Revolut's switching proposition from an ineligible e-money product into a full CASS member with FSCS protection, the only structural change of this kind among the UK neobanks in this set.**
For nine years Revolut operated without CASS access or FSCS protection. The PRA and FCA granted full banking authorisation on 11 March 2026, and CASS membership followed directly. Starling Bank and Monzo had held banking licences and CASS membership for several years before this; Revolut joins them as a peer rather than a near-bank alternative. The Czech Revolut entity retains its e-money licence and is outside the scope of this change. Sources: S-techcrunch-revolut-uk-banking-licence-pra-fca-ap, S-fintechcompass-revolut-uk-banking-licence-and-ca.

**Early Salary and CASS together produce the same salary-moment switching proposition Monzo built with Salary Sorter and Get Paid Early, confirming that first-salary capture is a converging standard among UK neobanks and not a single firm's product choice.**
Two independent UK neobanks have converged on the first incoming salary after a switch as the defining activation event. Revolut's mechanism is advance release; Monzo's is allocation into named pots. No incumbent in this set, Czech or UK, offers any feature targeting the same moment. Sources: S-moneyweek-revolut-bank-review-2026-and-cass-cove, S-pocketwise-revolut-uk-bank-account-review-2026.

**The migration of approximately 13 million UK customers from e-money accounts to full current accounts since March 2026 gives Revolut operational experience in managing account transitions at a scale no other participant in this set has faced.**
The migration is managed in tranches with approximately two weeks advance notice per batch. Account numbers, sort codes, IBAN and BIC are unchanged through the transition; transaction history carries over. Premium account holders receive new travel insurance policy numbers via email. This is the closest operational analogue in this market to the account-switching experience this run is addressing, executed at roughly 13 times the scale of Monzo's annual net CASS gains. Sources: S-moneyweek-revolut-bank-review-2026-and-cass-cove, S-en-revolut-article.

**Multi-currency at weekday interbank rate in 36 currencies positions Revolut on a dimension entirely absent from every other participant in this set, relevant to a customer segment that switches banks partly because no existing provider handles foreign income or spending without a margin.**
Starling charges 0.5 percent on foreign currency card spending above the free limit. Monzo charges 1.5 percent above GBP 200 per month. UK incumbents and Czech banks all charge materially higher FX margins. A customer receiving salary in a foreign currency or spending regularly abroad finds no equivalent proposition at any other bank in this set. Sources: S-bankinggeek-revolut-current-account-review-2026, S-pocketwise-revolut-uk-bank-account-review-2026.

### Where the journey stops

Card-on-file subscription payments sit outside CASS scope, as for every other participant in this set. Merchants store credentials against a card number rather than a bank account number, and no automated migration occurs on switching. Revolut offers no post-switch view of what payment instructions remain attached to a closed old account. Customer support operates exclusively via in-app AI chat with no phone channel on any tier; human agent response times range from a few minutes to several hours even on paid plans (S-bankinggeek-revolut-current-account-review-2026). Automated fraud detection triggers account freezes without timely case-specific explanation; multiple independent reviewers report accounts suspended for routine personal transfers, with support providing automated copy-and-paste responses rather than substantive updates (S-bankinggeek-revolut-current-account-review-2026, S-goodmoneyguide-revolut-customer-review-2026). The Czech Revolut product is an e-money account; Czech retail bank customers cannot use Revolut as a PAD-compliant inbound switching destination because the Czech Revolut entity holds an e-money licence rather than a Czech banking licence and does not participate in the Czech account switching service.

### What it costs

Standard: no monthly fee. Plus: GBP 3.99 per month. Premium: GBP 7.99 per month. Metal: GBP 14.99 per month. Ultra: GBP 45 per month. Annual billing is available at a discount of approximately 10 to 15 percent versus monthly rates. The Standard tier includes fee-free ATM withdrawals up to GBP 200 per month, weekday currency exchange on the first GBP 1,000 per month, and unlimited UK Faster Payments with no daily cap. Plus and above increase the FX allowance and reduce the weekend surcharge. Plus and above also include priority customer support routing and 24-hour human support access. Ultra includes comprehensive travel insurance, high-limit ATM allowances and enhanced savings vault interest rates.

### Confirmed not to do

Revolut offers no overdraft facility on the Standard plan and no credit card product on any plan; Banking Geek's feature review of the current account confirms both absences explicitly. No physical branch network exists and no web-only signup option is available; all account management is app-only, confirmed in Banking Geek's onboarding description. In-app chat is the only support channel and no phone support exists on any tier, confirmed in Banking Geek's customer service section. CASS participation and the associated FSCS protection apply to Revolut Bank UK Ltd in the UK only; the Czech Revolut entity holds an e-money licence and does not participate in the Czech PAD account switching service, confirmed in Fintech Compass's analysis of the banking licence and its geographic scope. No post-switch residual payment tracker or old-account visibility tool was identified in any review, help documentation reference or news article reviewed for this card; the absence was confirmed by examining Banking Geek's feature list, Pocketwise's review of the account, and MoneyWeek's coverage of the customer migration, none of which describe such a tool.
