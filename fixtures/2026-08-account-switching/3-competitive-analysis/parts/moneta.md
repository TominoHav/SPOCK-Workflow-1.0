## Moneta Money Bank

[rec:A6-r10]

Moneta Money Bank (formerly GE Money Bank, rebranded in May 2016) is the sixth-largest bank in the Czech Republic by market share, serving 1.6 million retail customers. It is listed on the Prague Stock Exchange (MONET), supervised by the Czech National Bank, and rated A1 by Moody's. The bank's stated strategy is to be the digital leader in the Czech retail market. The landscape map placed it as a leader for R1: the PAD switching obligation all Czech banks carry.

### What they offer

Moneta provides retail current accounts, savings products, consumer loans, mortgages, auto financing, leasing, debit cards, and credit cards to Czech retail customers. Smart Banka is the primary delivery channel; Internet Banka covers the same functions on desktop; 100-plus branches handle the remainder.

**Ucet Tom Plus current account charges 0 CZK per month with no qualifying spend condition and includes two free debit cards, free domestic ATM withdrawals, and free international ATM withdrawals.**
The account has no opening fee, maintenance fee, or closure fee. All domestic payments are free. The only exception is a fee of 0.1% of the amount (minimum 150 CZK) on foreign currency cash withdrawals. A fee schedule effective July 2026 is published on moneta.cz; all standard services remain free under it.

**Smart Banka consolidates over 200 functions and 41 products into a single mobile application available on iOS and Android, rated 4.8 out of 5 on the App Store and 4.7 out of 5 on Google Play.**
The app delivers full digital product origination: a customer can open an account, apply for a loan, and set up savings without visiting a branch. Internet Banka provides the same functions on desktop.

**Standing orders and direct debits can be created, modified, and cancelled entirely within Smart Banka without branch attendance.**
This is confirmed on the Smart Banka product page, which lists standing order and direct debit management as core in-app functions. A customer switching to Moneta can configure all recurring payment instructions from a smartphone at any point after account opening.

**Instant payments transfer funds within seconds, and QR code payments and international IBAN payments are available through the same interface, with all domestic transfers free of charge.**
The combination of instant payments and standing order management means a switching customer can complete the operational setup of a new account in a single app session.

**Virtual card capability supports Apple Pay, Google Pay, Garmin Pay, Fitbit Pay, and SwatchPAY for contactless payment by phone or watch without a physical card.**
Two physical debit cards are included with the account. The virtual card is configured inside Smart Banka alongside the physical card settings.

**Biometric authentication using facial recognition or fingerprint, QR code login, and PIN fallback are all available for app access, with encrypted data storage and desktop widgets for quick transactions and balance checks.**
These features are confirmed on the Smart Banka product page. The 4.8 out of 5 App Store rating reflects consistent user satisfaction with the access experience across the full feature set.

**Savings envelopes let customers ring-fence funds for named saving goals within Ucet Tom Plus without opening a separate savings product.**
The feature is integrated into Smart Banka. Dedicated savings accounts earning 3.50% on CZK balances (1.50% on EUR) are also available through the same interface for customers who prefer a separate product.

**Payment by phone number lets a customer send money to a recipient without sharing a full IBAN, integrated into Smart Banka as a standard payment method.**
This removes the need for the payee to share account details and reduces the steps required for first-time payments in the new account.

**A merchant reward programme offers up to 20% cashback on card payments within a named network of partner merchants, attached to Ucet Tom Plus and displayed inside Smart Banka.**
The eligible merchant list and active cashback rates are shown in the app. The reward is credited to the account on qualifying card payments at participating merchants.

**Consumer credit products include an optional overdraft (Flexikredit) from 2,000 to 100,000 CZK with no arrangement fee when set up online, plus consumer loans, mortgages, and auto financing, all accessible through Smart Banka or Internet Banka.**
Fixed-term deposits and savings accounts are available through the same platform. Moody's A1 credit rating and Czech Deposit Guarantee Scheme coverage to EUR 100,000 per person complete the full-service offering.

### What stands out

**Zero-fee current account with no qualifying conditions is the most transparent cost structure among the Czech banks profiled in this run.**
Moneta publishes a single, unconditional number: 0 CZK per month. This is confirmed by the Ucet Tom Plus product page on moneta.cz, the PickTheBank account comparison, and the duofinance.cz independent review, all of which note that no spending condition or minimum balance applies. Among the five Czech banks in this set, CSOB and Air Bank publish their full fee schedules in Czech only. KB has a fee-waivable structure that names qualifying conditions. Moneta's 0 CZK with no caveats is the plainest published number in the Czech field.

**Smart Banka's 200-plus functions and consistent 4.7 to 4.8 out of 5 app store ratings show a materially higher capability depth and user satisfaction score than the Czech peer average.**
The ratings are confirmed on the Smart Banka product page (moneta.cz) and in the duofinance.cz review. bankycr.cz describes Smart Banka as multi-award-winning. The 2025 results show digital platform users up 8.3% year-on-year and digital sales transactions up 24%, confirmed by bankycr.cz and the Yahoo Finance FY2025 earnings summary. The scope of functions in one interface, standing order management, instant payments, biometrics, savings envelopes, is not matched in equivalent English-language documentation by any other Czech incumbent in this set.

**Moneta deployed real-time marketing infrastructure in production, built with AWS, SAS, and Trask, that responds to a customer transaction within 30 seconds.**
The SAS April 2026 press release documents this as in production, with a 30-second response time measured from a triggering transaction to a push notification or in-app offer. Campaign deployment was reduced from weeks to hours. The system is integrated directly into the Smart Banka app and processes millions of transactions daily. It places Moneta in a position to respond at the moment a switching customer takes their first action in the new account, though no switching-specific use case is documented in public material.

**Net profit grew 12% in FY2025 to 6.5 billion CZK and total shareholder return exceeded 70%, confirming the digital investment has not come at the cost of financial stability.**
These figures come from the Moneta FY2025 earnings call highlights published on Yahoo Finance. The cost-to-income ratio fell below 42%, improving by 2.4 percentage points. Prague Stock Exchange listing and Moody's A1 rating add a level of public financial accountability that no Czech digital challenger in this set carries.

**Moneta's stated ambition to be the Czech market's digital leader is supported by measurable investment but does not yet extend to the account switching journey in English-language public material.**
The Accenture digital transformation case study, the SAS/AWS real-time marketing deployment, and the bankycr.cz product analysis all confirm significant digital spending. Against those metrics, the absence of any published switching timeline, customer obligation summary, or card-on-file handling documentation is the largest gap between the stated ambition and what a switching customer currently encounters.

### Where the journey stops

The switching service for Moneta accounts operates under PAD (Directive 2014/92/EU, Article 10, in force since September 2016) but no English-language documentation of the service scope, timeline, or customer obligations is publicly available. The research reviewed moneta.cz in English and found no equivalent of KB's "Switch your bank" page or NatWest's published Day 1 to Day 6 timeline. Czech-language switching documentation likely exists; it was not within the scope of this research.

Card-on-file payments (subscriptions and recurring charges stored by merchants against a card number) are outside PAD's scope by statute, not by Moneta's choice. No Moneta product or announcement describes a tool to identify or update card-on-file credentials after a switch. This is the same gap documented at every other participant in this run. Moneta has made no public claim to address it.

The Czech review site duofinance.cz notes that Moneta's foreign exchange rates for card payments in foreign currency are unfavorable compared to challenger banks. This is not a switching friction point in itself, but it is the clearest documented limitation of the account from an independent customer source, external to Moneta's own material.

The Accenture digital transformation case study covers 2022 to 2024. No product development above that baseline is documented in English-language public material for 2025 or 2026 at the product level. The real-time marketing infrastructure deployment (SAS, 2026) is documented, but it covers marketing execution and has no confirmed switching journey application.

### What it costs

Ucet Tom Plus: 0 CZK per month, no conditions. No opening or closure fee. All domestic payments free. Domestic ATM withdrawals free. International ATM withdrawals free. Two debit cards included. Foreign currency cash withdrawal: 0.1% of the amount, minimum 150 CZK. Flexikredit overdraft: 2,000 to 100,000 CZK, no arrangement fee when set up online.

Savings account: 3.50% on CZK, 1.50% on EUR (PickTheBank, 2026). Fixed deposits CZK: 3.40% for 1 month, 3.00% for 3 months, 3.10% for 6 months, 3.20% for 12 months. Fixed deposit EUR: 1.60% for 3 months. Minimum deposit for fixed products: 15,000 CZK or 500 EUR.

The full fee schedule is published in a Czech-language PDF on moneta.cz, with the current version effective July 2026. An English-language fee schedule was not publicly available at the research date.

### Confirmed not to do

An English-language switching service page covering what transfers, what the customer must handle, and a timeline was not found. The research reviewed moneta.cz for English-language switching content and found no equivalent of KB's "Switch your bank" page or NatWest's Day 1 to Day 6 schedule. Moneta's Czech-language materials almost certainly describe the PAD service; English documentation was not published as of 26 August 2026.

Card-linked payment migration is not offered. The review of moneta.cz product pages and all secondary sources in this set found no Moneta product, feature, announcement, or roadmap item describing a tool to identify or update card-on-file credentials held by merchants. PAD does not require it, and no participant in this run has deployed it. The absence is confirmed by the absence of any reference to it across ten sources covering product, investor, analyst, and customer perspectives.

AISP-enabled old-account payment visibility is unconfirmed. No Moneta documentation describes a feature that reads a switching customer's payment history from their old account via open banking and surfaces it in the new account interface. T3 is unconfirmed for all participants in this run. Moneta is not distinguished on this point relative to the others.
