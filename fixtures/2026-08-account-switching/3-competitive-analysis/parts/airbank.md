## Air Bank

[rec:A6-r07]

Air Bank is a Czech retail bank owned by PPF Group, founded around 2011 and serving 1.6 million customers by end of 2025, making it the fastest-growing bank in the Czech market over that period. It holds the KPMG Customer Bank of the Year award for nine consecutive years, a recognition no other Czech bank in this set has matched. The bank is selected to represent B2 (digital challengers using switching volume as a growth metric) and T3 (AISP-enabled old-account payment visibility for switchers); its specific switching proposition could not be confirmed from its primary site, which returned HTTP 404 on 26 August 2026.

### What they offer

Air Bank operates as a digital-first retail bank targeting Czech consumers who want a no-fee, app-centric banking relationship. Its product set spans everyday payments, multi-currency accounts, savings, investment, consumer lending, and mortgage services.

**Air Bank serves 1.6 million retail customers in Czech Republic and grew its customer base 49% between 2022 and 2025, the fastest customer growth rate among Czech banks in this set.**
The bank operates 32 branches and more than 1,000 employees, giving it a physical presence that pure neobanks do not have. Total assets grew 14.16% in 2025 and net sales revenue grew 24.33% in mid-2025. The customer acquisition rate reflects a structural shift toward digital-first banking among Czech consumers; at 2.03% market share it ranks 12th by assets, a small but rapidly expanding position relative to the three major incumbents. [S-ppf-ppf-group-annual-report-2025-air-bank-segmen, S-thebanks-air-bank-profile-czech-republic]

**The My Air mobile app is the primary service interface, providing real-time payment notifications, card controls, in-app customer service, and direct access to investment, lending, and savings products in one interface.**
Air Bank won the Golden Crown award for mobile banking in 2025. The app integrates Zonky (consumer lending), Portu (investment), and the Cvak payment feature. Staff at branches use tablets to assist customers rather than traditional teller terminals, a service model Air Bank introduced in 2024. A web-based internet banking interface exists but is secondary to the app experience. [S-ppf-ppf-group-annual-report-2025-air-bank-segmen]

**Air Bank integrates personal investment in US stocks and European ETFs directly into its retail banking app via the Portu platform, the first Czech bank to offer this at the primary account level.**
Customers invest from within My Air without opening a separate brokerage account or leaving the banking interface. Zonky peer-to-peer lending, part of Air Bank since 2021, is also accessible from the same app. Neither integration is replicated by CSOB, KB, or Moneta at an equivalent product depth within the primary account. [S-ppf-ppf-group-annual-report-2025-air-bank-segmen]

**Cvak is a contactless payment feature for Albert supermarket chain checkouts, reducing the number of card-on-file merchant credentials a customer accumulates over time.**
Customers who route regular Albert purchases through Cvak do not create a card-on-file entry at the merchant in the way a standard stored card payment does. In a switching context, a Cvak user has fewer merchant-held card numbers to update manually after moving banks. The feature is limited to the Albert chain and does not extend to other retailers or online merchants. [S-ppf-ppf-group-annual-report-2025-air-bank-segmen]

**Air Bank allows customers to hold up to 10 accounts simultaneously with zero maintenance fees and no qualifying conditions, including foreign-currency accounts in EUR and USD, since September 2025.**
All domestic payments are free. No minimum balance, minimum spend, or product bundling is required. This zero-fee structure means a customer can run an Air Bank account in parallel with their existing bank at no cost during a switching transition, which reduces the financial commitment of opening a new account before deciding to fully switch. [S-aroundprague-best-bank-in-czechia-air-bank]

**ATM withdrawals are free at terminals operated by Air Bank, UniCredit Bank, Moneta Money Bank, and Komercni banka, covering four bank networks under a shared ATM arrangement.**
The combined network exceeds what any single bank's own ATMs would provide. The specific total terminal count is not published in English-language sources. Withdrawals at banks outside this network incur fees that Air Bank does not state in English-language public material. [S-aroundprague-best-bank-in-czechia-air-bank]

**SEPA international payments are free, and non-SEPA international transfers cost a flat 100 CZK outgoing and 100 CZK incoming, below the Czech market typical minimum of around 200 CZK.**
The flat fee rather than a percentage-based model is a confirmed pricing differentiator. Most Czech banks charge a percentage fee on international transfers with a minimum around 200 CZK; Air Bank's 100 CZK flat rate applies regardless of the transfer amount, making it cheaper for moderate transfer values. [S-wise-air-bank-international-payment-fees-czechia]

**CZK fixed deposits earn 4.70% at instant access and 3.00% at the 3-month term; CZK savings accounts earn 2.60%, as of mid-2026.**
EUR and USD deposits earn 0.10%. These rates are published on the Pick the Bank comparison platform. The 4.70% instant-access rate is competitive with time-restricted deposit rates at Czech incumbents. Air Bank does not publish a full rate card in English on its own site. [S-pickthebank-air-bank-current-account-comparison-]

**All accounts are covered by the Czech Deposit Guarantee Scheme up to 100,000 EUR per depositor, the same statutory protection as the major incumbents.**
A customer moving from CSOB, KB, or Moneta to Air Bank does not take a step down in deposit protection. Deposit guarantee parity removes one potential barrier for a risk-aware customer evaluating the move. [S-pickthebank-air-bank-current-account-comparison-]

### What stands out

**Air Bank has been named Customer Bank of the Year in the KPMG CX Top 100 Brands ranking for nine consecutive years, a streak no other Czech bank in this set has matched.**
The KPMG CX award is independently administered and covers the full customer experience, not a specific product or channel. Nine consecutive wins establish Air Bank as the sustained CX leader in Czech retail banking. CSOB, KB, and Moneta have not published comparable independent CX recognition in English-language sources. The award does not specifically measure the switching experience, but the customer experience baseline from which Air Bank switchers arrive is the strongest evidenced in the Czech market. [S-kpmg-kpmg-cx-top-100-brands-2025-customer-bank-o, S-ppf-ppf-group-annual-report-2025-air-bank-segmen]

**Air Bank is the strongest candidate among Czech banks to deploy AISP-enabled old-account payment visibility for switchers, but no confirmed product or feature announcement for this capability was found in the research.**
Air Bank is selected in the landscape map to represent T3 because its pattern of digital innovation (investment integration, Cvak, tablet branches, sustained CX leadership) is the strongest evidence base among Czech banks for this type of capability. None of the sources found, including the PPF Group annual report and the Banky CR analysis, confirms that Air Bank has built or announced an open-banking-powered switch assistant. The T3 candidate status rests on an inference from capability pattern, not on a confirmed product. [S-ppf-ppf-group-annual-report-2025-air-bank-segmen, S-bankycr-air-bank-digital-strategy-and-product-an]

**Air Bank's Unity platform connecting its banking products with O2 mobile services, and its Portu investment integration, show a product strategy that extends beyond account and payments into telecoms and investment at the primary account level.**
Unity was launched in 2023 and allows Air Bank customers to manage banking and mobile services in a single relationship. This bundling reduces the switching incentives a competitor must match: a customer embedded in the Unity or Portu ecosystem has more of their financial life anchored in Air Bank than a customer who uses it for payments only. No Czech incumbent in this set replicates this bundling at the primary account level. [S-ppf-ppf-group-annual-report-2025-air-bank-segmen]

**Air Bank's zero-fee account with no qualifying conditions, combined with the shared ATM network, delivers the lowest confirmed total cost of basic retail banking among the five Czech banks profiled.**
The combination of zero maintenance, free domestic payments, free SEPA, and free cash access across four bank networks produces a verified zero-cost structure for a domestic-payment customer. Moneta's Ucet Tom Plus is also zero-fee but the shared ATM network Air Bank participates in is more extensive. KB and CSOB charge monthly fees with conditions. This cost position is a direct switching argument: a customer considering Air Bank faces no fee penalty during the evaluation period. [S-aroundprague-best-bank-in-czechia-air-bank, S-pickthebank-air-bank-current-account-comparison-]

### Where the journey stops

No English-language public material describes what Air Bank's account switching service looks like. The primary site (airbank.cz) returned HTTP 404 on 26 August 2026, so the bank's own description of its switching process is inaccessible. The Around Prague foreigners' banking guide describes Air Bank's features in detail and does not list a dedicated switching service or any above-floor switching feature among them. No English-language timeline, checklist, or process description for Air Bank's PAD-compliant switching service was found across any of the nine sources used.

The app and all contracts are in Czech only, which creates a structural barrier for non-Czech-speaking customers. A Czech phone number is required to open an account. Account activation requires either a payment from an existing Czech bank account in the customer's name or a branch visit, which means a customer arriving without any Czech banking relationship must visit a branch to get started. [S-aroundprague-best-bank-in-czechia-air-bank]

Trustpilot reviews show a 2.1 out of 5 overall score from 41 reviews, with 44% rated one star and 34% rated five stars. The negative reviews concentrate on language service failures (no English staff available, refusals to speak English despite staff fluency), unexplained service refusals citing the "geopolitical situation," and slow document processing. The 34% five-star cluster praises the app and no-fee structure. The split reflects a bank that performs well for digitally comfortable Czech speakers and poorly for non-Czech speakers who need human support. The English-language review pool is likely biased toward the expat experience, which amplifies language complaints relative to the overall customer base. [S-trustpilot-air-bank-customer-reviews-airbank-cz, S-aroundprague-best-bank-in-czechia-air-bank]

### What it costs

The main account costs 0 CZK per month with no qualifying conditions. All domestic payments are free. SEPA international payments are free. Non-SEPA international transfers cost 100 CZK outgoing and 100 CZK incoming. ATM withdrawals are free within the shared network covering Air Bank, UniCredit Bank, Moneta, and Komercni banka terminals; fees outside this network are not stated in English-language public material.

Since September 2025, customers can hold up to 10 accounts simultaneously, including foreign-currency accounts in EUR and USD, at no additional fee.

Savings rates: 4.70% on CZK instant-access fixed deposits, 3.00% on CZK 3-month fixed deposits, 2.60% on CZK savings accounts. EUR and USD deposits earn 0.10%. A full fee schedule in English is not published by Air Bank; the primary site was unreachable on the research date. [S-aroundprague-best-bank-in-czechia-air-bank, S-wise-air-bank-international-payment-fees-czechia, S-pickthebank-air-bank-current-account-comparison-]
