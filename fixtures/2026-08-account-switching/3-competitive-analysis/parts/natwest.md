## NatWest

[rec:A6-r04]

NatWest is a UK incumbent retail bank and the only participant in this set to publish a day-by-day switching timeline on its switch page. Its CASS service states explicitly what does and does not transfer, naming card-linked subscription payments and giving Amazon as the example merchant. In net CASS terms NatWest has been a losing institution in recent quarters, and its response in May 2026 was a £200 cash incentive, not a change to the post-switch experience.

### What they offer

NatWest provides personal current accounts across five tiers from free to £36 per month, all CASS-eligible, for UK retail customers.

**Full CASS switch completing in 7 working days, with a published Day 1 through Day 6-7 schedule.**
Days 1 to 2: the customer chooses a switching date and the old bank is notified. Day 3: the new NatWest account is established. Days 6 to 7: the account balance transfers and the old account closes. No other incumbent in this set publishes the switching process at this level of day-by-day detail.

**Automatic transfer of direct debits, standing orders, salary payments, and the existing account balance.**
All move without customer action as part of the CASS process. The balance arrives on Day 6 or 7, at the same time as old account closure. The payee list is also transferred.

**Indefinite redirection of incoming payments from the closed old account to the new NatWest account.**
Any payment sent to the old account after closure is forwarded automatically. NatWest states this service has operated for qualifying switches since September 2013 and publishes no end date.

**A published list of what the switch does not cover, naming card-linked debit card payments and streaming subscriptions explicitly.**
NatWest's switch page uses these terms: "Regular debit card payments - those that come from your card rather than the account, such as streaming subscriptions." Amazon is given as the example merchant where the customer must update card details manually "in the same way as you would now if your card expired." Mobile payment arrangements, open banking consents granted to third parties, and transaction history from the old account are also listed as outside scope.

**A Cora digital assistant that customers can use to confirm which direct debits have transferred.**
Cora is the post-switch verification tool NatWest names for the CASS process. It covers direct debit confirmation and does not extend to card-on-file merchants or subscriptions billed against a card number.

**A £200 cash incentive for eligible CASS switches to the Select or Reward accounts, available from May 2026.**
Conditions: pay in £1,250 within 60 days of switching and log in to the mobile app. The payment arrives within 30 days of meeting those conditions. NatWest offered £150 earlier in the same year before raising the amount.

**A mobile app showing all NatWest accounts in one view, with the ability to add external bank accounts for visibility.**
Basic open banking aggregation is available. This allows a customer to see balances at other providers alongside NatWest accounts, but does not read transaction data from the old account in the context of a switch.

**A current account range from free to £36 per month, with three intermediate tiers carrying insurance and lifestyle packages.**
Select Account at no monthly fee. Reward Account at £2 per month, with cashback on household bills paid by direct debit. Reward Silver at £10 per month with European travel insurance, breakdown cover and mobile phone insurance. Reward Platinum at £20 per month with worldwide family travel insurance. Premier Reward Black at £36 per month, requiring a £1,250 minimum monthly income deposit.

### What stands out

**NatWest is the only participant in this set to state the card-on-file exclusion in plain language on its switch page, with a named merchant as the example.**
Every CASS participant faces the same structural exclusion: card-on-file payments belong to the card credential held by the merchant, not to the bank account, and fall outside PAD's scope under R3. NatWest names this directly rather than leaving it for the customer to discover after the switch completes. The Amazon example is specific enough that a customer can identify their own equivalent and act before the switch rather than after. Which? and Banking Geek both confirm this level of explicit disclosure is not standard practice among UK incumbents. Czech banks in this set publish nothing equivalent in English-language public material (S-natwest-switch-to-natwest-page-including-explici; S-which-natwest-current-account-review-and-cass-da; S-bankinggeek-natwest-switching-service-review).

**The Day 1 through Day 6-7 schedule is the benchmark for completion-visible switching communication, and no Czech bank in this set publishes anything comparable.**
The schedule commits NatWest to a specific outcome on a specific day before the switch begins. O3 in this run asks for exactly this: a customer who can see the status of their switch as it progresses rather than waiting to find out when it is done. The Czech incumbent baseline, where no bank publishes a switching timeline in English-language public material, is the gap O3 is addressing. CASS data confirms the 7-day completion standard applies across all participating UK banks; what distinguishes NatWest is publishing the schedule day by day rather than stating the guarantee and leaving the steps implicit (S-currentaccountswitch-cass-net-switching-data-q3-; S-anna-uk-bank-switching-comparison-2026; S-natwest-switch-to-natwest-page-including-explici).

**The £200 switch incentive in May 2026, following a £150 offer earlier in the same year, shows NatWest measuring CASS outflows and choosing cash over product.**
Swift Money's 2026 comparison of UK switch incentives confirms NatWest escalated the amount within a single calendar year. Which? data confirm NatWest has been a net loser in CASS switches in recent periods. The pattern is escalating cash offers with no announced change to the post-switch journey, the same pattern as Lloyds Bank, and both incumbents are losing ground to challengers who built features around the salary-arrival moment rather than paying for volume. For this initiative, that pattern is the signal that incumbents have diagnosed the retention problem and reached for the cheaper answer (S-swiftmoney-best-bank-account-switch-offers-uk-20; S-which-natwest-current-account-review-and-cass-da; S-finder-natwest-current-accounts-2026).

### Where the journey stops

Card-on-file payments fall outside the CASS switch, and NatWest provides no tool for identifying which merchants hold the customer's old card details. The Cora assistant covers direct debit transfer confirmation and stops there. After Day 7 there is no NatWest feature that shows what is still pointing at the closed account. The customer must work through recurring charges as they surface on the new card rather than from a pre-populated list generated before the switch.

Customer reviews on Trustpilot show a recurring pattern of blocked cards and frozen accounts triggered by the unusual payment activity that is common in a switching period, with long waits for live support to resolve them. NatWest's digital-first service model routes these cases through Cora or the phone line. Branch access is declining: 18 sites are closing between 2026 and 2027, reducing the in-person fallback that has historically distinguished NatWest from digital challengers (S-trustpilot-natwest-customer-reviews-2026; S-finder-natwest-current-accounts-2026).

### What it costs

Select Account: no monthly fee. Reward Account: £2 per month, with cashback on household bills paid by direct debit from the account, which can offset or exceed the fee for regular bill payers. Reward Silver: £10 per month, includes European travel insurance, UK breakdown cover and mobile phone insurance. Reward Platinum: £20 per month, includes worldwide family travel insurance and European breakdown cover. Premier Reward Black: £36 per month, requires a £1,250 minimum monthly income deposit.

Arranged overdraft: 39.49% EAR variable, no default interest-free buffer. Foreign transaction fee: 2.75% on debit card purchases and ATM withdrawals outside the UK on the Select Account; premium tier rates differ. CHAPS payments incur a fee in the £20-25 range. No switch fee. No interest paid on current account balances at any tier.

### Confirmed not to do

NatWest's own switch page names these as outside CASS scope and the customer's responsibility to manage: card-linked debit card payments (subscription services and recurring charges stored against a card number rather than a direct debit or standing order), mobile payment arrangements, open banking consents granted to third parties, and transaction history from the old account.

NatWest does not provide a tool for identifying which merchants hold the customer's old card credentials before or after the switch. The Cora assistant was reviewed for this function and covers only direct debit transfer confirmation.
