## Starling Bank

[rec:A6-r01]

Starling Bank is the UK neobank that has gone furthest toward addressing the card-on-file problem structurally, without fully solving it. Its combination of virtual cards and named Saving Spaces isolates subscription credentials from the main account card, which reduces the manual update burden a CASS switch creates. At 4.6 million accounts with 10% annual growth and no cash switching incentive, the product is growing on the strength of the account itself. Starling makes no product intervention at the salary-arrival moment.

### What they offer

Starling provides a fee-free personal current account with a fully digital CASS switching journey to UK residents aged 18 and over. It holds a full UK banking licence, is regulated by the PRA and FCA, and offers FSCS deposit protection up to £85,000.

**Starling runs a full CASS switch inside its mobile app, with the old account closing automatically on switch day.**
The customer enters their old bank's sort code and account number; Starling handles the CASS mechanics from there. The switch completes within 7 working days and is all-or-nothing: no partial transfer option is available. Incoming payments redirect from the old account to the new one for a minimum of three years under CASS rules.

**Up to five virtual cards, each linked to a named Saving Space, let customers assign specific categories of spending to a separate card number.**
Each virtual card is free to create and functions as a standard Mastercard debit card. A customer who routes subscriptions through a dedicated Subscriptions Space and virtual card sees all recurring charges in one place, and those merchant relationships are attached to the virtual card number rather than the main account card. The virtual card works independently of the main account card, so changes to one do not affect the other.

**Saving Spaces divide the account balance into named sub-accounts without requiring a separate sort code or account number.**
Each Space earns up to 4.50% AER interest on the balance held within it. Spaces serve both savings goals and spending categories. The virtual card feature makes any Space into a funding source for a distinct card number, combining budgeting and payment separation in one structure.

**The personal current account charges no monthly fee, no UK ATM fee, no overseas ATM fee and no foreign transaction fee.**
Faster Payments within the UK are free. Cash deposits at Post Office branches are free up to £10,000 per day with immediate clearing. Card replacements, whether standard or lost or stolen, carry no charge. There is no minimum deposit and no minimum balance requirement.

**An arranged overdraft is available at 15% EAR, charged daily and billed monthly.**
No banding applies; one rate covers all eligible customers. Transactions that would exceed the arranged limit are declined rather than incurring an unarranged overdraft charge. The specific rate and the absence of tiering are both stated publicly on the fees page.

**Spending Intelligence, an AI-powered tool launched in January 2026, analyses transaction patterns and surfaces categorised spending insights inside the app.**
The tool extends Starling's existing transaction categorisation, adding pattern detection and natural-language queries over spending history. A gamified balance-awareness feature called Last Digit Widget was released at the same time as part of a broader money management suite.

**24/7 UK-based phone support is available to all personal current account holders.**
Monzo and Revolut handle most customer contact through in-app chat. Starling maintains a phone channel around the clock for all account types. Three independent review sources covering 2025-2026 rank Starling as the most phone-accessible of the three major UK neobanks.

**FSCS deposit protection covers balances up to £85,000 per person.**
Starling holds a full UK banking licence and is regulated by the PRA and the FCA. Deposit protection places Starling on the same regulatory footing as Lloyds Bank and NatWest within this profile set, and equal to Monzo and Revolut.

### What stands out

**Virtual cards linked to Saving Spaces create a structural path to reducing card-on-file credential exposure during a CASS switch, and no other participant in this profile set offers the same combination.**
A customer who assigns subscriptions to a dedicated virtual card before switching retains those merchant relationships on an instrument that is independent of the main account card. When the CASS switch changes the main card number, the virtual card number is unaffected, and the merchants attached to it require no credential update. No other bank in this set combines a digital-first switching journey with a pre-switch subscription isolation tool. The feature requires deliberate setup by the customer and does not migrate existing merchant relationships. Sources: `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`, `S-moneytothemasses-starling-bank-current-account-r`.

**Starling has grown to 4.6 million personal accounts at 10% annual growth without offering cash switching incentives.**
Its August-October 2026 switch offer gives new customers completing a CASS transfer within 30 days a SURI Pro 2.0 electric toothbrush, with a retail value of £105, rather than cash. Monzo and NatWest use cash offers in the same period. Growth at this rate without cash payments is the strongest available indicator that the product itself drives switching, rather than incentive spend. Source: `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`, `S-en-starling-bank-article`.

**The arranged overdraft rate of 15% EAR is the lowest among the three UK neobanks profiled in this set.**
Monzo's arranged overdraft runs from 19% to 39% EAR depending on the customer. NatWest charges 39.49% EAR. Starling applies one rate to all eligible customers without banding, making overdraft pricing transparent and predictable. Source: `S-pocketwise-starling-bank-fees-uk-2026`.

**24/7 UK phone support distinguishes Starling from Monzo and Revolut within the UK neobank set.**
Three independent review sources covering 2025-2026 rank Starling as the most accessible of the three for human contact. The phone channel is available regardless of account type or issue, and is described by Good Money Guide reviewers as the most responsive of the three. Sources: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`, `S-moneytothemasses-starling-bank-current-account-r`, `S-moneyweek-starling-bank-review-and-account-compa`.

### Where the journey stops

Card-on-file subscriptions are outside CASS scope and require manual credential updating after the switch. Starling's own switching documentation confirms this: customers must update card details stored by merchants in the same way as they would if their card expired. The virtual card feature reduces this problem for customers who set it up before switching. It does nothing for customers who did not, and does nothing for existing merchant relationships already attached to the main card.

Trustpilot records over 46,000 customer reviews for Starling, and three recurring failure patterns appear across them: accounts closed without advance notice or explanation, transactions blocked without an immediate customer verification opportunity, and customer service calls that fail to resolve issues. One reviewer describes a 32-minute call with no outcome. Source: `S-trustpilot-starling-bank-customer-reviews`.

Good Money Guide reviewers note that customers held in Starling's account review process can be blocked from accessing their own funds for multiple days, with information requested sequentially rather than in a single step. Source: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`.

The FCA fined Starling £29 million in October 2024 for anti-money laundering control failures, described in the FCA's public statement as "shockingly lax." The bank paid the fine and remains operational. The heightened screening that the AML remediation introduced provides the context for the elevated rate of account restrictions and transaction blocks visible in customer complaints. Source: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`.

Starling removed interest from the main personal current account balance in February 2025. Interest is now only available within Saving Spaces. Customers who hold a working balance in the main account earn nothing on it. Source: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`.

No physical branches. Cash deposit options are limited to Post Office branches rather than ATMs or retail locations. Source: `S-goodmoneyguide-starling-bank-review-2025`.

### What it costs

Personal Current Account: no monthly fee, no minimum deposit, no minimum balance. UK ATM withdrawals are unlimited and free. Overseas ATM withdrawals are unlimited and free worldwide, with no fair-use cap or daily limit beyond the account balance. Foreign card payments carry no transaction fee, using the Mastercard network rate. Faster Payments transfers within the UK are free. Cash deposits at Post Office branches are free up to £10,000 per day. Standard and lost-or-stolen card replacements are free.

Arranged overdraft: 15% EAR, charged daily and billed monthly. Unarranged overdraft: not offered. Transactions beyond the arranged limit are declined.

International transfers: a conversion fee is built into the displayed exchange rate. The specific percentage is not published on the public fees page; the fee is shown in the transfer flow before the customer confirms.

Business Current Account: £7 per month. EUR business account: £2 per month as an add-on. Bulk Payments add-on: £7 per month.

Source: `S-pocketwise-starling-bank-fees-uk-2026`.

### Confirmed not to do

Card-on-file migration at the point of switch is not offered. Starling's own switching documentation states that customers must update card details stored by merchants manually after the switch completes. The virtual card feature addresses pre-switch setup only; it does not contact merchants on the customer's behalf. Source: `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`.

Salary sorting is not available. Monzo's Salary Sorter divides incoming salary automatically across named Pots on arrival, and Monzo's own product page documents this feature. Starling's features pages list no equivalent. Saving Spaces can be funded manually but do not respond automatically to salary arrival. Sources: `S-monzo-cass-switching-page-get-paid-early-page-sa`, `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`.

Early salary release is not offered. Monzo's Get Paid Early releases salary 24 hours before standard clearing, and Revolut's Early Salary operates the same mechanism. Starling's product pages list no equivalent feature. Sources: `S-monzo-cass-switching-page-get-paid-early-page-sa`, `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`.

Employer salary notification is not part of the switching journey. Starling's switching page confirms that updating salary payment instructions with the employer is the customer's responsibility. Source: `S-starlingbank-account-switching-help-page-and-fea`.
