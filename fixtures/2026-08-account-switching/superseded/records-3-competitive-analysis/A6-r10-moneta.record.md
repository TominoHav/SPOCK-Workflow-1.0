## What they offer

**Ucet Tom Plus: 0 CZK monthly fee with no qualifying spend condition.** The headline retail current account has no monthly charge and no minimum spend waiver mechanism. Free ATM withdrawals worldwide are included in the base account.

**Smart Banka: full digital banking on iOS and Android with biometric authentication.** The app supports FaceID, TouchID, instant payments (Okamzite platby), and full online product origination. Moneta describes Smart Banka as a "paperless experience," meaning new products can be opened and existing ones managed without visiting a branch or using paper documents.

**Internet Banka: web portal parallel to Smart Banka.** Customers who prefer a desktop interface can use the web portal alongside or instead of the mobile app.

**PAD-compliant account switching service.** Moneta, as a Czech credit institution, is required to offer switching under PAD. The service scope is confirmed by regulation; the customer-facing documentation and interface details were not found in English-language public material.

**Instant payments (Okamzite platby) built into the standard account.** A payment standard present across all five Czech banks profiled; not a differentiator but a prerequisite for retail banking competitiveness.

**SME banking alongside retail banking.** Moneta describes itself as a leading provider in both the retail and SME segments. The retail proposition and the SME proposition share the same digital infrastructure.

## What stands out

**Moneta states "digital leader" as a strategic aim.** The investor relations homepage names this as an organizational goal: "to be a leading Czech bank and the digital leader in the Czech market." The Smart Banka investment is the primary evidence. Whether this ambition extends to the switching journey specifically is not confirmed.

**0 CZK monthly fee with no conditions is a stronger base retail proposition than KB at the base tier.** KB's base account carries conditions for fee waiver; Moneta's does not. For a customer primarily concerned with switching cost overhead, Moneta's fee structure is simpler.

**The Accenture case study positions the digital transformation as a 2022-2024 achievement.** By 2026, Smart Banka is the delivered product. Any further product development above that baseline is not confirmed in publicly accessible material.
