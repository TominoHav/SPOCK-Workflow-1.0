## What they offer

**PAD-compliant account switching service.** CSOB, as the largest Czech credit institution by retail customer count, is required to offer switching under PAD. The service scope is confirmed by regulation; the customer-facing documentation and process details were not found in English-language public material.

**4.34 million retail customers.** The largest Czech retail bank by customer count, with 280 branded branches and 3,300 Czech Post agency branches. KBC Group subsidiary with access to group-wide digital banking technology and risk management infrastructure.

**Camunda process automation adopted for cross-channel unification.** Announced in 2024, Camunda was selected for process orchestration and channel consistency across CSOB's retail operations. Whether the switching customer journey is within scope of the Camunda deployment is not disclosed in public material.

**1,805 billion CZK in assets.** The largest Czech bank by total assets. KBC Group's Czech operation contributes materially to KBC Group's Central and Eastern Europe segment results.

**Digital banking with mobile and internet channel.** CSOB offers retail banking through a mobile app and internet banking portal. The depth of digital features for switching is not confirmed in English-language public material.

**Czech Post agency network: 3,300 points.** The agency agreement with Czech Post gives CSOB a distribution reach that no digital-only challenger can replicate. This matters for the switching journey to the extent that customers use branch channels to complete the switch.

## What stands out

**CSOB's English-language switching documentation is zero.** The bank offers the switching service; no English page describes it. This is the baseline across the Czech market, and CSOB illustrates it most clearly because its scale means switching customers are more likely to arrive from or depart to CSOB than any other Czech bank.

**The Czech Post network represents a structural distribution advantage.** 3,300 agency points is not a product feature but a distribution moat. For customers who want a branch-assisted switching journey, CSOB's reach is unmatched in the Czech market.

**Camunda investment signals an intention to improve operational efficiency across channels.** Whether that includes the switching journey is not confirmed. The investment is the most concrete evidence of process improvement ambition among the Czech banks in this set.
