## What they offer

**1.6 million customers by end of 2025.** Grown from 1.07 million in 2022 (49% in three years). PPF Group subsidiary. The growth rate is materially above the Czech retail banking market average, confirming Air Bank's customer acquisition advantage.

**9th consecutive KPMG CX Top 100 Brands award as Customer Bank of the Year.** The only bank in this competitive set with an independent, consecutive CX recognition at that frequency. The award is sourced from KPMG's annual Czech survey of consumer brands; it is not self-awarded.

**My Air app: investments in US stocks and European ETFs directly inside the banking account.** A depth of product integration not offered by CSOB, KB, or Moneta at the primary account level. The investment feature is co-located with the current account rather than in a separate product.

**Cvak contactless payment at Albert supermarket tills.** Customers who pay regular Albert purchases via Cvak (a QR/NFC payment method at Albert) rather than a stored card have fewer card-on-file credentials to update in a future switch. This is a structural side effect of the payment method, not a switching feature.

**PAD-compliant account switching service.** As a Czech credit institution, Air Bank is required to offer switching under PAD. The service scope is confirmed by regulation; the customer-facing switching documentation and interface details were not found in English-language public material.

**Digital-first bank: no legacy branch network.** Air Bank operates without the branch infrastructure of the Czech Ring 1 incumbents. Customer service and account management are primarily digital with telephone support.

## What stands out

**Air Bank has the best-documented customer experience track record of any Czech bank in this set, by a significant margin.** Nine consecutive awards from KPMG is an independent, repeated measurement of customer preference. No other Czech bank in this set has a comparable external validation at this frequency.

**Air Bank is selected to represent T3 (AISP-enabled old-account visibility).** Its digital depth and CX orientation make it the most likely Czech bank to pursue open banking capabilities for the switching journey. T3 is not confirmed as a current or roadmap feature; Air Bank is the candidate, not the confirmed holder.
