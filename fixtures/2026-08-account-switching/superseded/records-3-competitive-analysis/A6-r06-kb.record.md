## What they offer

**Dedicated English-language "Switch your bank" page.** The only Czech bank in this competitive set with a published English-language switching page. The page states what transfers automatically and names what the customer must do manually. This is the most transparent switching documentation of any Czech bank in this set.

**Automatic transfers: standing orders, direct debits, payment orders with future maturity, old balance.** KB confirms these transfer automatically. The customer does not need to set up new standing orders or direct debit mandates; KB coordinates their transfer.

**Manual customer actions stated explicitly: notify employer, update invoices and regular clients, contact utilities, update payment templates.** KB names the residual obligations the switching scheme does not cover. This honest statement of the PAD gap is unique among Czech banks profiled here.

**KB+ unified app: personal and business accounts, payments, investments, and loans in one interface.** Migration to KB+ takes 10 minutes online with a mobile phone. The app integrates personal and SME banking alongside retail products.

**Temenos banking platform.** The Temenos core banking system underpins KB's digital product delivery. Societe Generale holds 60% of KB; the remaining 40% is publicly traded.

**Old account closure on request.** KB does not close the old account automatically; the customer must request closure. This is relevant for customers who follow B1 (maintaining both accounts as insurance) or are concerned about the residual card-on-file gap.

## What stands out

**KB names the customer's remaining obligations in English: employer, utilities, invoices.** No other Czech bank in this set does this in English. The transparency is honest about the gap but does not reduce it. The customer knows scope but not schedule.

**No switching timeline is published.** KB confirms what transfers and what does not, but does not state how long the switch takes or what happens on each day. NatWest's Day 1 to Day 6-7 schedule is the gap between KB's transparency and the UK documentation standard.

**60% Societe Generale ownership and Temenos platform give KB access to group digital banking capability.** Whether that capability is applied to the switching journey is not confirmed.

## Where the journey stops

No switching timeline stated. Card-on-file payments are not mentioned: neither transfer nor explicit exclusion is stated. No tool to identify which payees need updating. No post-switch view of old-account payment activity. The customer knows what to do but not when it will be done or how to find every merchant that needs updating.

## What it costs

KB+ account: fee structure available in Czech and English. Standard account has a monthly fee with conditions for waiver. Full pricing at kb.cz. No switch fee is charged for the switching service itself.

## Confirmed not to do

Does not transfer card-on-file subscription credentials (PAD scope; not stated explicitly on the switch page). Does not provide a post-switch view of what is still pointing at the old account. Does not notify the customer's employer automatically. Does not publish a day-by-day switching timeline.
