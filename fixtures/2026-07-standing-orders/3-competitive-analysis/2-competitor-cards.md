# Competitor Cards -- Standing Order and Recurring Commitment Management

[rec:R-A6-01]

Eleven participants were profiled against the market scope across two research rounds: standing order creation, modification and cancellation in retail banking, with subscription management and recurring card payment control as adjacent capabilities. The first round (R-A6-01) profiled eight participants; the second round (R-A6-02) profiled the three that the first round's 8-participant bound could not reach. No S1 cards from prior runs were reusable; all existing cards were researched against a different market scope. All eleven cards reached six independent publisher_keys.

## CSOB (csob.cz)

### What they offer

**Full retail banking suite.** CSOB provides current accounts, payment cards, savings, investment products via CSOB Asset Management, and FX conversion through its CSOB SmartBanking internet portal and CSOB Smart mobile app. [S-csob-wiki, S-csob-banksdaily]

**Standing order management (trvalé príkazy).** Available in the CSOB SmartBanking online portal for creation, modification and cancellation. The primary portal (online.csob.cz) returned HTTP 500 throughout this research and in the prior run; the self-service flow cannot be confirmed from CSOB's own documentation. [S-csob-wiki, S-csob-smart-app]

**Payment card controls in the CSOB Smart mobile app.** The Czech-market CSOB Smart app covers temporary card block, permanent cancellation, spending limit management and card unblocking without branch contact. [S-csob-smart-app]

**Instant and international payments.** CSOB SmartBanking supports domestic and SEPA credit transfers; FX conversion is available via the CSOB Smart mobile app. [S-csob-wiki, S-csob-banksdaily]

**Multi-currency accounts and FX services.** Available to personal and business customers through online banking and the app. [S-csob-wiki]

**Commercial and SME lending, digitized.** In March 2025 CSOB selected the nCino Platform to modernize its commercial and SME lending operations, aiming for faster lending decisions and deeper client insights. [S-csob-ncino]

**190+ branches and 1,200+ ATMs.** CSOB maintains one of the largest branch and ATM networks among Czech banks, with 5,000-9,999 employees supporting retail and corporate clients. [S-csob-banksdaily, S-csob-emis]

### What stands out

**CSOB is the largest bank in the Czech Republic by total assets** (CZK 1,953 billion as of March 2024; net profit CZK 15.4 billion in 2024), making it the primary scale reference point for any Czech digital-banking benchmark. [S-csob-wiki]

**Euromoney named CSOB Best Bank in Czech Republic in 2024 and 2025**, citing digital transformation as part of the award rationale. [S-csob-euromoney]

**KBC Group ownership (100% since 2007) and active digital investment.** The nCino selection in March 2025 confirms CSOB is extending digital transformation into commercial lending, not only retail channels. [S-csob-wiki, S-csob-ncino]

### Where the journey stops

The primary digital banking portal (online.csob.cz) returned HTTP 500 on all access attempts during both the 2026-06-instant-payment-disputes run and this run. The standing order self-service flow, subscription management scope and fee structure are unconfirmable from CSOB's own documentation. No dedicated subscription management or recurring card payment visibility feature was found across secondary sources. Standing order management appears to be confined to the web portal; the CSOB Smart app covers card and payment management but no standing order or subscription workflow is described in the App Store listing.

### What it costs

Standard current account fees apply. Specific fees for standing order creation or modification were not confirmable from available sources (primary site inaccessible throughout).

### Confirmed not to do

No dedicated subscription management hub or recurring card payment cancellation flow confirmed in either the CSOB Smart app or the SmartBanking portal. Primary site unreachable across two runs (primary_unreachable: true).

## KB (kb.cz)

### What they offer

**KB+ as the primary digital banking interface.** KB+ is the current-generation mobile app covering payments, account overview, card management, FX conversion and ten savings envelopes; Mobilní Banka (legacy app) remains available. [S-kb-appstore-plus, S-kb-mwm]

**Standing orders (trvalé príkazy) fully self-service, digital-only since January 2024.** KB eliminated paper standing order submission at branches as of 1 January 2024; the digital channel is now the only path. Customers configure the amount, scheduled date, payment symbols and a custom name; orders are modified or cancelled at any time in MojeBanka or KB+. Setting up, modifying and cancelling a standing order is free for all customers. [S-kb-cards-page, S-kb-sipo-faq]

**SIPO payment management.** KB's FAQ confirms self-service SIPO setup -- the Czech collective invoicing system for utility payments -- via online banking. SIPO is distinct from a general subscription management capability. [S-kb-sipo-faq]

**Direct debit (inkaso) registration and management.** Documented in KB's standing order and direct debit FAQ; manageable online without branch contact. [S-kb-sipo-faq]

**365 branches, 714 ATMs and 452 deposit machines.** KB maintains one of the broadest physical networks in the Czech Republic alongside its digital channels. [S-kb-banky]

**Tap to Pay launched February 2025.** KB extended contactless payment capability with the Czech launch of Tap to Pay, indicating continued investment in payment innovation. [S-kb-globaldata]

### What stands out

**KB was the first Czech incumbent in this cohort to publicly confirm the migration to a digital-only standing order channel.** Paper instructions at branches were eliminated as of 1 January 2024 -- a concrete operationalization of the digital-first narrative, confirmed in KB's own customer communications. [S-kb-cards-page]

**KB's FAQ is the most explicit documentation of recurring payment types among Czech incumbents**, covering standing orders, SIPO and direct debit registration in a single resource, with modifications free for all customer types. [S-kb-sipo-faq]

**Société Générale Group ownership (60.35%) and active core banking investment.** KB's 1.6 million customers benefit from SG's development investment; the 2021 Temenos Transact core banking selection signals long-cycle digital infrastructure work now approaching maturity. [S-kb-wiki, S-kb-globaldata]

### Where the journey stops

No subscription management layer or recurring card payment visibility was found in KB's public documentation. SIPO is Czech utility billing only, not a general subscription management capability. Standing order management covers the traditional bank-initiated payment type only. No independent review source describing the user-facing standing order experience was found; the mwm.ai analysis noted user complaints about loading errors when cancelling a standing order, suggesting the feature exists but may have reliability issues.

### What it costs

Standing order setup, modification and cancellation: free for all customers, confirmed in KB's FAQ. Current account fees apply; KB's fee levels are noted as higher than Czech internet banks. [S-kb-sipo-faq, S-kb-banky]

### Confirmed not to do

No subscription tracking, recurring card payment management or merchant-level payment visibility confirmed.

## Revolut (revolut.com)

### What they offer

**Smart Subscriptions for Czech customers.** Revolut's Czech interface (revolut.com/en-CZ/subscriptions) provides full visibility of all subscriptions, direct debits and recurring payments in one place. Users receive notifications when a payment is due or funds are insufficient, and can block a specific subscription amount or block a merchant entirely. Blocking does not cancel the subscription -- cancellation requires contacting the merchant directly. [S-revolut-finextra-search, S-revolut-wiki]

**Virtual cards in two types for Czech users.** Multi-use virtual cards (permanent details, suitable for recurring subscriptions) and disposable cards (single-transaction, auto-deleted after use) are both available. Czech users can hold up to 20 active virtual cards, with a maximum of 4 new cards created within 30 days. [S-revolut-wise]

**Multi-currency accounts covering 36+ currencies.** Revolut provides live exchange rates and multi-currency balances, reducing FX costs for subscriptions billed in foreign currencies. [S-revolut-wiki, S-revolut-coinlaw]

**Standing orders for EU IBAN accounts.** Scheduled recurring credit transfers are supported for accounts with a Revolut European IBAN. [S-revolut-wiki]

**Czech banking licence since 2021.** Revolut operates as a licensed bank in the Czech Republic, not as a prepaid card provider, giving Czech customers deposit protection under Czech law. [S-revolut-coinlaw, S-revolut-wiki]

**Tiered plans.** Standard (free), Plus, Premium and Metal -- with Revolut Ultra introduced for the Czech market as of 2024. [S-revolut-wiki]

### What stands out

**Smart Subscriptions is the most explicitly named subscription management product in this cohort**, built into the Czech Revolut app with a dedicated interface that surfaces all recurring charges in one view and links to block controls -- proactive visibility without requiring the customer to search. [S-revolut-finextra-search, S-revolut-wiki]

**Revolut generated £3.1 billion in total revenue and £1.1 billion in pre-tax profit in 2024**, with subscription revenues alone reaching £423 million (74% YoY growth), confirming that a subscription management model at scale is commercially significant. [S-revolut-techeu]

**52.5 million global customers in 2024** -- Revolut added 15 million new users in a single year, making it the largest player in this cohort by user count and indicating strong adoption of the neobank subscription management model. [S-revolut-techeu, S-revolut-coinlaw]

### Where the journey stops

All primary Revolut web properties (help.revolut.com, blog.revolut.com, revolut.com/news, revolut.com/en-CZ/subscriptions) returned HTTP 403, making it impossible to confirm the current detailed scope of Smart Subscriptions from Revolut's own documentation. The key distinction -- that blocking a payment does not cancel the underlying subscription -- comes from the Czech interface description accessed via search; its full terms are not independently confirmed. The depth of standing order management for Czech accounts is also not confirmed from accessible sources.

### What it costs

Standard (free), Plus, Premium, Metal and Ultra tiers. Smart Subscriptions availability by tier and specific pricing for Czech accounts was not confirmed from accessible sources.

### Confirmed not to do

Primary source unreachable throughout research (primary_unreachable: true). Direct cancellation of subscriptions inside the app is not available -- users must contact the merchant; Revolut blocks payments but does not cancel mandates. SEPA direct debit management scope for Czech accounts is not confirmed.

## Monzo (monzo.com)

### What they offer

**Direct debit cancellation self-service, entirely in-app.** Monzo allows customers to cancel any direct debit mandate from the app without calling the bank or visiting a branch; a list of all active direct debits is visible and each can be individually cancelled. [S-monzo-dd-cancel]

**Virtual cards with auto-delete for free trial management.** On Extra and above (£3/month), customers create virtual cards each linked to a specific Pot; cards can be set to auto-delete on a specified date, providing a mechanism to end free trials before they convert to paid subscriptions. Cancelling the virtual card cuts off the recurring charge. [S-monzo-virtual-cards, S-monzo-finder]

**Bills Pot for ring-fencing committed spending.** The free-tier Bills Pot allows customers to set aside money for direct debits and standing orders separately from discretionary spending; balances are funded automatically on payday via Salary Sorter. [S-monzo-finder]

**Salary Sorter for payday allocation.** Income received is automatically distributed across Pots (bills, savings, spending) according to preset rules, separating committed from discretionary money on arrival. [S-monzo-finder]

**Spending analytics and merchant categorisation.** Monzo provides a transaction feed with merchant categorisation, supporting visibility into recurring payment patterns. [S-monzo-tradingcritique]

**Ranked number one for personal current-account service quality.** Monzo holds the top position in the UK FCA's independent Service Quality survey, with 93% of surveyed customers saying they would recommend it. Finder rates it 9.3 out of 10. [S-monzo-finder]

### What stands out

**Monzo's direct debit cancellation is native, in-app and requires no branch visit or phone call** -- the mandate is cancelled immediately from the direct debit list, with no agent-assisted step required. This is the benchmark for recurring payment self-service among the UK neobanks. [S-monzo-dd-cancel]

**Virtual cards with auto-delete create a frictionless free-trial management mechanism** -- a customer sets an expiry date when creating the virtual card and it deletes itself at that date, ending any subscription tied to it without any action at cancellation time. This proactive control is absent from all Czech participants in this cohort. [S-monzo-virtual-cards, S-monzo-tradingcritique]

**FY2025 revenue reached £1.235 billion** (48% growth year-on-year) with Monzo recording its first annual profit (£15.4 million, year to March 2024) -- confirming that in-app direct debit cancellation and virtual card controls can anchor a profitable retail banking proposition. [S-monzo-pymnts]

### Where the journey stops

Monzo is a UK bank only and is not available to Czech retail customers. Its direct debit management covers BACS mandates only; Czech SEPA direct debits are outside scope. The subscription management capability is a combination of direct debit lists and virtual cards, not a proactive subscription detection hub. Bills Pot requires a payday deposit route that may not apply to Czech banking patterns.

### What it costs

Basic (free), Extra (£3/month, virtual cards included), Perks (£7/month), Max (£17/month). All GBP-denominated.

### Confirmed not to do

Not available in the Czech Republic. No Czech banking licence. Direct debit management is UK-BACS only.

### What it costs

N26 Standard: free. N26 You: EUR 9.90/month. N26 Metal: EUR 16.90/month. Standing order management included in all tiers at no additional charge. [S-n26-standing-orders]

### Confirmed not to do

No proactive subscription detection or merchant-level card payment management confirmed. N26 is not currently accepting new Czech Republic customers.

## Tapix by Dateio (tapix.io)

### What they offer

**Recurring Payments Intelligence API.** Tapix's core product identifies, classifies and enriches recurring transaction data at approximately 1.5 billion transactions per month, with 99.99% stated accuracy at approximately 3-millisecond processing latency. [S-tapix-main, S-tapix-rpi]

**Subscription Management for Banking Apps.** A white-label UI module that banks embed in their own apps, surfacing subscription detection, recurring payment categorisation and cancel-initiation to end customers, without the Tapix brand being visible to consumers. [S-tapix-sub-mgmt]

**Coverage of all recurring payment types through one API.** Tapix covers card subscriptions, pattern-based recurring transactions, standing orders and direct debits -- described as "no single-network blind spot" -- enabling a bank to surface every commitment type in one view. [S-tapix-rpi]

**Merchant resolution at 700,000+ merchants and 2 million+ locations across 112+ markets.** Each transaction is enriched with merchant logo, accurate brand name, GPS, business category, Google Places data, carbon footprint and website URL. [S-tapix-main, S-tapix-sourceforge]

**Mastercard AN4569 compliance support.** Tapix helps banks meet Mastercard's network requirements for enhanced merchant data display. [S-tapix-sourceforge]

**Cost-per-API-call cloud-based pricing.** Tapix operates as a transaction-volume SaaS with no upfront licensing; banks pay for enrichment volume at a rate disclosed in commercial engagement. [S-tapix-sourceforge]

### What stands out

**Tapix is the only pure infrastructure participant in this cohort** -- its product is the technology layer that enables subscription management inside any bank's app. A Czech bank could license Tapix rather than build enrichment and subscription management from scratch, with a Prague-based vendor relationship, shared regulatory context and no currency risk on procurement. ## Calling the bank (substitute:contact-centre-call)

### What they offer

**Agent-mediated standing order cancellation, effective within 48 hours.** When a customer calls the bank or visits a branch, a human agent processes a standing order cancellation with a legal effectiveness guarantee: UK payment rules require banks to process the instruction within 48 hours to prevent the next scheduled payment from going out, and this applies across all major Czech and UK banks that offer this channel. [S-sub-gocardless]

**Continuous Payment Authority (CPA) cancellation by absolute legal right.** Under UK and EU payment law, customers hold an unconditional right to cancel any recurring card payment authority by contacting their bank. The bank must process this request even if the merchant objects, and the customer does not need to resolve the dispute with the merchant first. The FCA and the Financial Ombudsman confirm this right applies to all UK-licensed banks. [S-sub-gocardless, S-sub-fca]

**Agent escalation for disputed or fraudulent recurring charges.** The phone channel provides access to trained agents who can review transaction histories, initiate disputes for unauthorised recurring charges, apply emergency card blocks for fraud, and raise formal complaints. These tasks require human judgment and are consistently retained in the human channel even as banks automate simpler queries with AI. [S-sub-mosaicx]

**Multi-channel reach: phone, branch, and in-app chat.** The contact centre is accessible via telephone, branch visit, and digital chat. In the Czech market, KB operates its call centre 7:00am-10:00pm weekdays and 8:00am-10:00pm weekends; Ceska sporitelna provides telephone banking via SERVIS 24 for payment management. 46% of customers choose to call first when they have a real problem, making telephone the primary escalation route for payment management issues. [S-sub-fca, S-sub-bankrate]

**No technology barrier -- accessible to all customer segments.** The phone channel requires no app, no online banking registration, and no digital literacy. It is the default access route for customers without smartphones, older customers, and customers in low-connectivity areas -- a population that digital-only recurring payment management tools systematically exclude. [S-sub-fca]

**Agent advice on contractual obligations and notice periods.** An agent can check whether a subscription carries a contractual notice period or early termination penalty and advise the customer before cancelling the payment -- a service that a self-service cancellation flow typically does not provide. [S-sub-gocardless]

### What stands out

**The right to cancel any Continuous Payment Authority from the bank is absolute and unconditional under UK and EU payment law**, meaning the contact centre is the channel that enforces a legal right the customer holds regardless of the merchant's behaviour. No other channel in this cohort can make this claim, because the right belongs to the banking relationship rather than to a feature the bank built. [S-sub-fca, S-sub-gocardless]

**46% of UK consumers still choose to call their bank first when they have a real problem**, confirming that the contact centre is not a legacy holdover but an active channel that a plurality of customers consider their primary resolution route for payment management issues -- making the channel's deficiencies a direct driver of customer dissatisfaction that digital features exist to address. [S-sub-fca, S-sub-bankrate]

**The contact centre is the only channel available for complex or contested recurring payment scenarios** -- disputes, fraud, multi-party recurring obligations, and contractual advice that requires accessing account history and escalating to compliance or collections processes. Banks that invest in digital subscription management consistently report that AI and self-service channels handle simpler tasks but complex payment problems reach human agents. [S-sub-mosaicx]

### Where the journey stops

Wait times are the defining friction point. More than half of UK bank customers report their bank is too slow to respond. The experience of navigating automated phone menus before reaching an agent -- for a task that digital-first banks now handle in two to four taps -- is a strong driver of migration to neobank products. The FTC recorded nearly 70 consumer complaints per day in 2024 about recurring subscription cancellation difficulties (up from 42 per day in 2021), a portion of which involves call-channel friction compounding merchant-side resistance. [S-sub-fca, S-sub-ftc]

The contact centre provides reactive control only. Customers must discover an unwanted charge before they can call; the channel offers no proactive visibility of upcoming recurring payments, no notifications of price changes, and no consolidated view of all commitments. The 73% of consumers who want to manage all subscriptions in one place cannot do that through a series of phone calls to individual banks. [S-sub-fintechtimes]

Cancelling a recurring payment via the bank does not cancel the underlying contract. Customers who stop a payment by calling the bank still owe the merchant for the subscription period and may receive demands or debt collection contact. The agent's role is payment control, not contract management. [S-sub-gocardless]

### What it costs

No monetary cost to the customer for using telephone or in-branch banking. The customer's cost is time: call wait times, call duration (typically significant for payment management issues), and branch travel time where applicable. Banks absorb call centre operating costs internally. Czech banks charge no fee for standing order cancellation via telephone banking. [S-sub-bankrate, S-sub-gocardless]

### Confirmed not to do

The contact centre does not provide a dashboard or consolidated view of recurring payments. Agents cannot see subscription charges before they occur and cannot send proactive notifications of price changes. The channel cannot pause, downgrade, or renegotiate a subscription -- only block or cancel the payment to the merchant. Research by Minna Technologies (2024) found that 80% of consumers prefer adjusting plans over cancelling outright and 86% would consider a retention offer rather than cancel -- neither option is available via a bank phone call. [S-sub-fintechtimes]
