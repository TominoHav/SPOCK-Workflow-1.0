# Problem material: goal-based saving

Four items, supplied by the user. **This is the quarantine boundary.** Nothing below is a finding and nothing below has been reduced; the observations are one document over.

## Customer 1, retail client

Customer 1, 31, has a current account and a savings account she opened two years ago. The savings account holds 800 CZK.

> "I opened it because they asked me to when I got the mortgage quote. Then I never put anything in it. It's not that I don't want to save. I just never decide to."

Asked what she is saving for: "A car, eventually. But that's not a plan, that's a thought."

Asked whether she moves money to it: "Once. On the day I opened it."

## Customer 2, retail client

Customer 2, 38, keeps everything in the current account and has no savings product.

> "I know roughly what I need to keep for the flat and I know the rest is mine. But it's all one number, so the rest doesn't feel like the rest, it feels like everything."

Asked what would change that: "If some of it looked like it was already spoken for. Not locked. Just labelled."

Asked about a savings account: "That's a whole thing. Another account, another statement, and then I'm moving money back and forth."

## Customer 3, retail client

Customer 3, 27, uses a round-up feature at a different bank and holds her main account here.

> "The round-ups are the only saving I do that actually works, and it's because I don't do it."

Asked whether she knows how much is in there: "Not exactly. I look every few months and it's more than I expected, which is the nice part."

Asked why her main account is here: "Salary, mortgage, everything. I just don't save here."

## Savings team

The savings team reports that a large share of savings accounts opened in the last two years hold under 1,000 CZK, and that most of those had one deposit and nothing after it.

The note carries no figure. It says the pattern is visible in the product reporting and that nobody has pulled the number.

## What we could not establish

Whether the pattern in the savings team's note holds at the size the note implies. **No number was supplied and the note says so itself.**

Whether Customer 3's experience of round-ups at another bank generalises. One person is one person.
