# Problems | Prioritised opportunities

**Two opportunities go forward as primary, two as secondary and one is parked.** The order came from the trust tag and nothing else, because not one of the five carries a client count. [rec:R-A4-01]

If a count existed the order would have been built on it and might have been different. That is stated rather than hidden, because the reader is entitled to know what the ordering rests on.

## What we are solving first

**O1, there is no moment at which the client decides to save.** Two origins, three qualifying references, all first-hand. It is the primary opportunity of this run.

**O2, the second account is itself the reason not to.** The other opportunity with two independent origins. It describes the same gap from the product's side rather than the client's, and the two together are why the run is about saving inside the current account rather than beside it.

## What comes after

**O3, committed money and spare money look the same.** One origin, specific and testable, and unconfirmed. It is carried because the client rejected locking explicitly, which distinguishes it from a savings product and from a spending limit.

**O4, an account opened at a prompt gets one deposit.** One qualifying origin. The relayed half is where the only claim about scale sits, and the note says nobody pulled the number.

## What we are not solving now

**O5, automatic saving works by not being watched.** Parked, with what would complete it written down: ask clients who save automatically whether they want progress shown or available.

**It is parked because it argues against the other four, not because it is weak.** Four opportunities point towards showing a client their progress; this one says the saving works because she does not look. Dropping it would remove the only thing in the material that disagrees.

## How we decided the order

Trust, and nothing else. Two opportunities stand on two origins with a first-hand reading, and go first. Three stand on one origin, and follow.

**No opportunity carries a market size and none should.** That question belongs to the competitive analysis stage, which runs after this gate and produces exactly that material.

## What we could not establish

How many clients any of this affects. The one place a number exists is the savings team's product reporting, and their own note says nobody queried it. That is carried forward unresolved rather than closed.

Whether O1 and O3 are one opportunity. They are kept apart because one client raised each and neither raised the other.
