## ID-1

**Strategic alignment: 25 of 30, Moderate.** Removing the search step is one step from SHD-4, the average time a banker spends daily on administration. The material puts half a day per client into assembly and twenty minutes into abandonment; both are administration in the key result's own terms. It scores 25 rather than 30 because the chain holds only where the sources are connected, and whether they can be is a purpose-limitation question nobody has answered.

**Difficulty: nothing scored.** No sub-dimension scored. Compliance Guidelines and Banking Constraints are both absent, so nothing here weighs cost, technical load or regulatory risk.
