# Ideas | Selected Idea: ID-1

ID-1, one question box that answers who do we know for this across every business line and every entity record the bank holds, was selected at the decision gate on 20 August 2026 by the Product Owner. It answers H1, the problem that the bank's knowledge of a person is split across boundaries nobody searches across, and it is the precondition for the two problems placed above it. **The selection was made under a standing instruction to take the first option and no reason was stated for it**, which is recorded as it happened rather than reconstructed. [rec:R-A9-01]

The scores put ID-5 above it, at 30 of 30 against 25, on the only axis that could be scored. That disagreement is left standing rather than resolved here.

---

## What was selected

**ID-1: Ask once.** A single retrieval surface over the bank's own record, scoped to people and organisations, that answers an attribute question across the whole institution rather than within one system or one reporting line. It resolves a client group's several legal entities into one subject before it answers, so a question asked about a client is not answered against a sixth of it.

## What it solves

**H1, and H1 is the only problem it solves.** The bank's knowledge of a person is split across business lines, systems and entity records that nobody searches across, so a route that exists inside the institution is invisible to the person who needs it. Three independent first-hand origins name three different kinds of wall: a business line with its own reporting line, six unlinked entity records inside one client group, and a sister system credit cannot read.

## Why this one

**No reason was given and none is invented.** The Product Owner set a standing instruction before the stage ran that every decision in this run takes the first option, and the selection arrived under that instruction. A rationale written here would read at stages 5 and 6 as though a person had weighed the five ideas, and that is the one thing this artifact exists to keep straight.

**What can be said without inventing anything** is what the other artifacts already establish. ID-1 is the precondition for the two problems the prioritisation placed above H1, and the prioritisation says so in as many words: solving H3 without H1 gives a product specialist faster access to the same partial picture. The bank also already holds the entity resolution twice, in credit for exposure and in compliance for ownership, which makes this an assembly rather than a build.

## What it does not address

**H2, the substance behind a name, is not in this idea.** ID-2 carried it and was not selected. The problem with no market answer at all, and the one the whole landscape is organised around, is not the problem this pilot answers.

**H3, the access constraint, is only reached indirectly.** The brief's success signal is a sentence about H3 and this idea does not answer it. It makes an answer possible for whoever asks; it does not remove the need to ask a relationship manager.

**H5, provenance, is untouched**, and that matters more here than it looks. Four of ten profiled participants surface people with a score and no evidence, all three interviewed users rejected exactly that, and a retrieval surface that returns names without provenance is the same failure with the bank's own logo on it.

**H4 and H6 are untouched**, which is expected: H4 was placed second and H6 is parked.

## What this carries into the next stage

**The three properties that make it this idea rather than a search box.** One question asked once, with the institution deciding where to look. Group resolution before retrieval rather than after it. And a stated answer where a source could not be searched, rather than a shorter list.

**One warning, carried from the competitive synthesis.** Zero of ten participants supply provenance behind a surfaced person and the users will not act without it. If stage 5 scopes a pilot that returns names alone, it will produce the market's failure faster. The provenance decision belongs in the pilot even though H5 is not the problem being solved.

**The constraint that arrives before any of it.** Purpose limitation decides which of the bank's own systems can be repurposed, and it applies from the day a source is connected.

## What we could not establish

**Why this idea rather than ID-5.** The scoring places ID-5 first on the only axis that could be scored, the Product Owner selected ID-1 under a standing instruction, and no reason was given. Both facts are on the record and neither explains the other.

**What any of this costs.** No Banking Constraints body exists for this run, so the difficulty axis scored zero of zero on all five ideas and the selection was made on value alone.

**Which sources can actually be connected.** The idea assumes the bank's systems can be read together and nothing in this run establishes that they may be.
